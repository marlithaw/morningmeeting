My boundary & helping toolkit

Ask → Wait → Respect mini-card

Front:

ASK “Can I ____?”

WAIT Listen.

RESPECT “Yes,” “No,” and “Not now” are answers.

Back:

IF SOMEONE NEEDS HELP

SAY IT “Stop. Hands off.”

GET HELP Find an adult.

CHECK IN “Are you okay?”

Direct only when safe. Get adult help for ongoing harm. Checking in afterward does not replace getting help now.
