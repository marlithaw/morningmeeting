Our classroom covenant

Respect bodies. Protect privacy. Act safely. Repair harm.

OUR BODILY AUTONOMY & DIGNITY COVENANT

WE OWN OUR BODIES. We respect another person's right to control their body, clothing, and personal space.

WE PROTECT PRIVACY. We do not turn someone's embarrassment, exposure, or distress into entertainment.

WE ACT WHEN HELP IS NEEDED. We use safe bystander choices and involve adults when needed.

WE REPAIR HARM. When we cross a boundary, we stop, own our action, complete appropriate repair, and change the pattern.

This is what belonging requires: your place in our community remains intact, and our standards remain intact too.
