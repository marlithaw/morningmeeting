THE BOUNDARY ANALYSIS CARD

Permission What observable evidence shows agreement?

Dignity Is someone's body, clothing, privacy, or embarrassment becoming entertainment?

Safety Could this create physical risk or activate a threat response?

Crowd Are observers increasing or reducing the harm?

ACCOUNTABILITY TEST

Before calling it repair, ask:

Did I name my action accurately? Did I remove excuses? Did I complete the repair expected of me? Can my future behavior show change?

Direct only when safe. Get adult help for ongoing harm. Checking in afterward does not replace getting help now.

Practice card

THE ACCOUNTABILITY TEST

Instead of: “It was just a prank. Nobody got hurt.”

Try: “I crossed a body boundary and made another person's private space public. That was my choice. I will follow the repair plan and change how I act around their body and space.”

Test it: Does it name the behavior? Remove excuses? Address repair? Identify change?
