My boundary & helping toolkit

Permission · Dignity · Safety Decision Card

1. PERMISSION Did the person clearly agree?

2. DIGNITY Does the action protect their body and privacy?

3. SAFETY Does the action keep their body and environment safe?

If one answer is NO → Stop and choose another action.

Back:

THE 4 Ds

DIRECT — Say what needs to stop. DISTRACT — Break the momentum. DELEGATE — Get adult help. DELAY — Check in afterward.

Direct only when safe. Get adult help for ongoing harm. Checking in afterward does not replace getting help now.
