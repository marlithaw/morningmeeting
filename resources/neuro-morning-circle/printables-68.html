Lesson 1 · Emotional Temperature Check · Grades 6–8

Biometric Narratives & Glimmer Mapping

I can: I can scan my body for stress and name a “glimmer” that signals safety in this room.
Somatic set-up: Settle into the chair, feet solid on the floor. Close eyes or look down. Starting at the toes, notice any tightness. Move up the legs, belly, chest, neck, jaw. Locate where stress is sitting in your biology.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Sits in a quiet body-scan posture, feet flat, body steady.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the biometric chart on the board (Heart Rate, Muscle Tension, Breath).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Discusses body stress points and room safety anchors with a partner.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Tenses shoulders, neck, and jaw, then releases them on cue.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Uses a Chromebook or tablet to submit a specific regulatory commitment.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions to the math block, carrying focused energy.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Scan the QR code to post your commitment on our digital board: ‘Today I will protect my safety by [specific action].’”
Lesson 2 · Mindful Breathing · Grades 6–8

Box Breathing with Beats

I can: I can sync my breath to a steady beat to regulate my heart rate and settle my nervous system.
Somatic set-up: Sync breathing to a metronome beat (60 BPM). Breathe in for 4 beats, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat for 4 full cycles to stabilize autonomic arousal.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Sits upright, chest open, syncing respiratory cadence to the metronome.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Faces the board, tracking the visual box-breathing diagram.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares breath experiences using sentence frames with a partner.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices biometric self-awareness, monitoring heart-rate drops.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Types a specific, observable commitment into the digital form.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Moves instantly to seats. Quick transition under 45 seconds.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Submit your digital commitment: ‘I will use box breathing if I feel hyperaroused today.’ Clear specificity.”

Lesson 4 · Daily Intention Setting · Grades 6–8

Executive Function Blueprinting

I can: I can build a blueprint that re-engages my focus and own my next step under stress.
Somatic set-up: “Stretching, Twisting, Yawning” (STY) sensory breaks. Sway the shoulders, gently twist the torso side-to-side, and stretch the hands wide to discharge cortisol.
