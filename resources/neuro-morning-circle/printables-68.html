Lesson 1 · Emotional Temperature Check · Grades 6–8

Check-In: Reading My Early Warning Signals

I can: I can read my early warning signals and name where stress shows up in my body before it takes over.
Somatic set-up: Sit tall, feet flat. Close your eyes or look down. Take a slow breath and scan from your shoulders to your hands to your stomach. Notice one place that feels tight and one place that feels calm.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Sits still, breathes slowly, and scans the body for tension.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the check-in scale on the board (Calm, Okay, Tense, Overloaded).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares a personal stress signal with a partner using the sentence frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Tenses and releases the fists and shoulders, noticing the change.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific, doable plan on a card and posts it.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions on the cue, moving calmly and quietly.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “On your check-in card, finish this: ‘When my early warning signal shows up, I will ___.’ Post it where you can see it.”
Lesson 2 · Mindful Breathing · Grades 6–8

Box Breathing

I can: I can use box breathing to slow my heart rate and calm my body when I feel stressed.
Somatic set-up: Trace a square in the air or on the desk. Breathe in for 4 as you trace up, hold for 4 across the top, breathe out for 4 down, hold for 4 across the bottom. Repeat four times.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Traces the square and matches the breathing count.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Follows the box-breathing diagram on the board.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares how their body felt using the sentence frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Runs one round of box breathing independently, hand on chest.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific moment when they will use box breathing.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and moves calmly and quietly on the cue.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “On your card, write: ‘I will use box breathing when I ___.’ Name a real moment today.”
Lesson 3 · Cultural Wisdom & Shared Stories · Grades 6–8

Perspective & Respect

I can: I can take another person’s perspective and disagree respectfully without putting them down.
Somatic set-up: Cross your arms and give your own shoulders a gentle squeeze. Breathe in, feel the squeeze, and let your shoulders drop as you breathe out.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Does the shoulder squeeze with slow breathing.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the respect frames on the board (Agree · Add on · Disagree).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares a real example of feeling respected using the frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices disagreeing respectfully with the sentence frame and a calm tone.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific, doable respect commitment.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions calmly and quietly on the cue.

Value of the day: Respectful
My commitment: “On your card, finish: ‘Today I will show respect by ___.’ Make it something you can actually do.”
Lesson 4 · Daily Intention Setting · Grades 6–8

Know Your Needs, Own Your Next Step

I can: I can name what’s draining my focus and choose a tool and a next step to stay on track.
Somatic set-up: Do a quick 5-senses reset. Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you can taste. Then take one slow breath.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Runs the 5-senses reset, anchoring attention to the present.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the “What’s draining my focus?” list (sleep, food, stress, conflict).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Names a personal drain on focus using the sentence frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Chooses and rehearses a specific tool from their toolkit.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific, doable next-step commitment.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions calmly and quietly on the cue.

Value of the day: Responsible
My commitment: “On your card, write: ‘Today I will own my next step by ___.’ Name one small, real win.”
