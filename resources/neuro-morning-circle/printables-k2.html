Lesson 1 · Emotional Temperature Check · Grades K–2

Body Weather Report

I can: I can notice my “body weather” and know that every feeling is safe in our circle.
Somatic set-up: Sit with a tall spine, both hands flat on the tummy. Close eyes or look at shoes. “Breathe into your hands. Feel your tummy rise and fall. Is it warm and calm like a sunny day, fluttery like a wind gust, or tight like a stormy cloud?”

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Places hands on stomach; sits quietly and mimics breathing.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Points to the weather chart on the board (Sun, Clouds, Rain, Storm).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Receives ball, speaks weather zone using visual supports, or passes ball.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Shakes hands vigorously, then freezes completely, releasing muscle tension.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Whispers commitment to a partner or draws weather state.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Chants “On the one!” and walks slowly, keeping a regulated physical pace.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Today, I commit to being Safe. If my weather turns stormy, I can draw a picture of a sun on my paper. Whisper your safe commitment.”
Lesson 2 · Mindful Breathing · Grades K–2

Five-Finger Breathing Beats

I can: I can use my breathing hand to slow my body down whenever my engine runs too fast.
Somatic set-up: Hold out one hand like a star. Using the index finger of the other hand, trace up the thumb on the inhale, pause at the tip, and trace down on the exhale. Repeat for all five fingers.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Sits tall, traces the hand, focuses on tactile input and slow breath.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Repeats the definition of safe: keeping ourselves and our friends from harm.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Turns to a partner and shares using the simplified sentence starter.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices bilateral gross-motor coordination integrated with breath.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Traces the hand or draws a star on a sticky note, posting it on their workspace.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands instantly and transitions to their seats without vocalization.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Draw a quick hand-star on your sticky note. Place it on your desk as your commitment to use your breathing tool today.”
Lesson 3 · Cultural Wisdom & Shared Stories · Grades K–2

The Kindness Echo

I can: I can send a kind echo and show respect so everyone knows they belong in our circle.
Somatic set-up: Place the right hand over the chest to feel the heartbeat. Turn the chin to the left and take a deep breath, then to the right and exhale. Model a warm, relaxed facial expression.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Keeps a hand on the chest, sways gently side-to-side, stabilizing breath.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Listens to the definition of Respectful: protecting our classroom’s shared voice.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Whispers the kind echo to a peer, using passing gestures.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices turning shoulders fully and making brief, comfortable eye contact.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Whispers a commitment: “I will use kind words when someone drops their pencil.”

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions to their seats, carrying a posture of respect.

Value of the day: Respectful
My commitment: “Today, I commit to being Respectful by… whisper your kind commitment to our echo partner.”
Lesson 4 · Daily Intention Setting · Grades K–2

My Learning Superpowers

I can: I can choose a Learning Superpower and be Responsible for my next step today.
Somatic set-up: “Grounding Roots Stretch.” Stretch hands all the way up like tall trees reaching for the sun, take a deep breath, then exhale and press both feet flat into the carpet.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Performs the grounding roots stretch, focusing on balance and weight.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Looks at the visual superpower chart (Listening Ears, Helping Hands, Calm Breathing).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Points to a visual selection, sharing their chosen daily focus.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices saying “My bad, I’ll fix it” with a neutral facial expression.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Draws a symbol of ears, hands, or spiral breath, showing the circle.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Moves silently to their seats on the call-and-response cue.

Value of the day: Responsible
My commitment: “Draw your superpower symbol on your commitment sheet. Hold it up to commit to yourself.”
