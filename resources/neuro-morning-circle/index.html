Neuro-Regulated Morning Circle

A 15-minute trauma-responsive Morning Circle that regulates the body before instruction begins. Every 15-minute lesson runs the same six-step arc: Regulate, Orient, Connect, Practice, Commit, and Transition.

Regulation guardrails

Regulate before you reason. Warm Demander logic balances unconditional positive regard with absolute procedural accountability. When a student is pushed outside the Window of Tolerance, de-escalate the biology (Regulate) before addressing accountability (Reason). If a student shares a current concern of harm, hunger, or crisis, respond with warm empathy, protect privacy, do not process it in front of peers, and route the student privately to support services the same day.
