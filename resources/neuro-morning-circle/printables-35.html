Lesson 1 · Emotional Temperature Check · Grades 3–5

Safety Thermometer & Early Warning Signals

I can: I can find my early warning signals and read my Safety Thermometer before I boil over.
Somatic set-up: Place two fingers on the neck or wrist to locate the pulse. Close eyes and count beats for 10 seconds. “Is your heartbeat steady and cool, or is it racing up the thermometer?”

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Tracks pulse, sitting upright with feet flat on the floor.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the Safety Thermometer board (Blue, Green, Yellow, Red).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares physical indicators with a partner using the target sentence frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Tenses facial and hand muscles, then completely relaxes them, releasing tension.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific, observable coping commitment and posts it.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Transitions instantly on the verbal cue, moving silently.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Write on your Toolkit slip: ‘If my heart rate goes up, I will…’ Pin it to your commitment board.”
Lesson 2 · Mindful Breathing · Grades 3–5

Calm-Down Sound Anchor

I can: I can use a calming sound to stretch my exhale and quiet my brain’s smoke detector.
Somatic set-up: Play a short calming, kid-friendly clip (soft music or nature sounds — see the linked clip). Close eyes and listen, letting each exhale grow longer as the sound plays. When it ends, raise a hand and notice how your body feels.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Sits in calm silence, listening to the clip with eyes closed.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Looks at the brain diagram on the board showing the Amygdala.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Discusses sensory-motor focus strategies with a circle partner.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Matches slow breathing to the rise and fall of the calming clip.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific commitment incorporating a breathing or listening anchor.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and transitions silently, carrying a calm physical posture.

Value of the day: Safe
My commitment: “Write on a sticky note: ‘Today I will stay in my Window of Tolerance by…’ and post it on our circle board.”
Lesson 3 · Cultural Wisdom & Shared Stories · Grades 3–5

The Perspective Lens

I can: I can use a perspective lens and respectful bridging phrases to protect someone else’s experience.
Somatic set-up: Place hands on opposite shoulders, giving a gentle, reassuring squeeze. Breathe in through the nose, feel the squeeze, and let the shoulders drop on the exhale.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Performs the shoulder squeeze with steady breathing sways.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Reads the “Three E’s” board (Event, Experience, Effect).

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Shares positive, evidence-based peer respect stories using the talking piece.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Practices listening actively and using respectful bridging phrases.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes a specific perspective-taking commitment and posts it on the board.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands instantly and transitions cleanly to academic tables.

Value of the day: Respectful
My commitment: “Commit: ‘Today I will protect someone’s experience by…’ Write it on a toolkit card.”
Lesson 4 · Daily Intention Setting · Grades 3–5

Pothole Filling & Intention

I can: I can name a “pothole” shrinking my window and choose a tool to fill it today.
Somatic set-up: “5-Senses Grounding Tool.” Sit flat. Find 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste.

The six-phase circle

Regulate · 2 minutes

Calm the nervous system

Practices 5-senses sensory grounding, anchoring attention.

Orient · 2 minutes

Name today’s value

Looks at the “Pothole Filling” poster showing sleep and nutrition.

Connect · 3 minutes

Share · relational capital

Identifies personal stressors and shares with a partner using the frame.

Practice · 3 minutes

Rehearse the move

Rehearses selecting and applying somatic tools from the coping toolkit.

Commit · 3 minutes

Own a specific intention

Writes an observable commitment and tapes it to their desk space.

Transition · 2 minutes

Stand on the one

Stands and walks smoothly, avoiding physical contact or chatter.

Value of the day: Responsible
My commitment: “Write your pothole commitment: ‘If my window shrinks, I will use [tool].’ Post it on your desk.”
