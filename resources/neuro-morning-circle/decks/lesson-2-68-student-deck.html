Box Breathing with Beats

Use a disciplined, synchronized metronome cadence (box breathing) to regulate heart-rate variability and settle the central nervous system.

I can: I can sync my breath to a steady beat to regulate my heart rate and settle my nervous system.

Regulate

Breathe with me“Settle your body. Listen to the metronome. Breathe with the beat. Inhale 1-2-3-4. Hold 1-2-3-4. Exhale 1-2-3-4. Hold…”

Somatic move: Sync breathing to a metronome beat (60 BPM). Breathe in for 4 beats, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat for 4 full cycles to stabilize autonomic arousal.
