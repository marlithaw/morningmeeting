Box Breathing

Use a steady, even breathing pattern (box breathing) to calm the body and mind.

I can: I can use box breathing to slow my heart rate and calm my body when I feel stressed.

Regulate · 2 minutesCalm the nervous system

Regulate

Breathe with me“Let’s breathe in a square. Trace up—breathe in, 2, 3, 4. Hold across, 2, 3, 4. Down—breathe out, 2, 3, 4. Hold, 2, 3, 4.”

Somatic move: Trace a square in the air or on the desk. Breathe in for 4 as you trace up, hold for 4 across the top, breathe out for 4 down, hold for 4 across the bottom. Repeat four times.
