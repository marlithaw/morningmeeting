Check-In: Reading My Early Warning Signals

Build body awareness by noticing early stress signals — racing heart, tight muscles, shallow breath — before they escalate.

I can: I can read my early warning signals and name where stress shows up in my body before it takes over.

Regulate

Breathe with me“Settle in. Feet flat, shoulders down. Slow breath in… and a longer breath out. Quick body scan—where are you holding stress today?”

Somatic move: Sit tall, feet flat. Close your eyes or look down. Take a slow breath and scan from your shoulders to your hands to your stomach. Notice one place that feels tight and one place that feels calm.
