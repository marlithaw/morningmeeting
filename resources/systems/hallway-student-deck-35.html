Matchbook Culture Camp · Hallway TravelGrades 3–5 · 07

Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

“Look at these doors. Behind every one of them somebody is trying to concentrate. The hallway is the only place where how you act affects people you never even see.”

Look for

  • Students look at the doors
  • Students name the invisible audience

If it breaks

  • Have them stand outside a working classroom and listen.

Support adult

  • Teach in the hallway during instructional time so the point is real

Language support

  • The physical setting carries the meaning.

Visual: Line-up icon. Teach in the actual hallway with doors visible.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“In for four, out for six. The breath OUT is the one that calms you — that is the whole trick. Three rounds.” Count aloud with them.

Look for

  • Exhale visibly longer
  • Shoulders drop by round two

If it breaks

  • “This one is for you, not for me. Eyes down, one more round.”

Support adult

  • Co-regulate beside students who struggle

Language support

  • Counting is the only demand.
  • Model rather than describe.

Visual: Lungs and calm-face icons; optional 4/6 count bar.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Voice off — not quiet, off. Single file on the right. Walking pace with no gaps, because gaps are where it falls apart. And nothing touched on the way past.”

Look for

  • Voice fully off rather than whispering
  • Gaps closing without prompting

If it breaks

  • “Stop. Close the gaps. We restart from here.”

Support adult

  • Position an adult at the back where gaps form

Language support

  • Four icons carry the standard.

Visual: Four expectation icons. Post at hallway corners.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“Name the exact spot where our line falls apart, and why. Corners and stairs are usually it, because you lose sight of each other.”

Look for

  • Specific locations named
  • Causes identified rather than blame

If it breaks

  • Walk the route and let them point to the break points.

Support adult

  • Station adults at the named break points

Language support

  • Word bank names the locations.

Visual: Two-people icon with word bank of actual locations.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“We walk the real route, including the corner where it breaks. Timed as a class against our own run. Two rounds.”

Look for

  • The named break point holding
  • Time improving

If it breaks

  • “Stop. Back to the corner. Again.”

Support adult

  • Stand at the break point for round one

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal is fully accessible.

Visual: Three icons plus clock. Walk the actual route students use.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Name the behavior and the exact spot. 'In the hallway' is too vague — name the corner.”

Look for

  • A specific location named
  • Behavior matches the location's failure mode

If it breaks

  • “Which spot is hardest for you?”

Support adult

  • Notice it at that spot tomorrow

Language support

  • Word bank covers the first blank.

Visual: Check icon.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“How we leave the meeting is how we enter the learning. We time it as one class, against our own record — not against each other.”

Look for

  • Chairs in unprompted
  • No pushing or racing

If it breaks

  • “Reset. First thirty seconds again until it is clean.”

Support adult

  • Redirect to the step, not the behavior

Language support

  • Icons carry the sequence.
  • Post the class time — numbers are accessible.

Visual: Four step icons plus a clock showing the class's previous time.
