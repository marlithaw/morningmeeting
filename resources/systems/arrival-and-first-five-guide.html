Entering and First Five Guide

Audience: Teachers, teacher assistants, leaders, substitutes

This K–8 guide covers the classroom entry routine and the first five minutes after a group enters a new class. K–2 and grades 3–5 use it when transitioning to electives and other instructional spaces; grades 6–8 use it throughout class-period changes. The purpose is to make each opening calm, relational, and academically ready before anything else competes for attention.

Scope: This is not the buildingwide Morning Entry procedure and it is not end-of-day Dismissal. The paired updated procedure covers how students enter and exit the classroom; First Five is the immediate academic on-ramp after they enter.

Quick Spec

Element Specification
Cue / signal Teacher at threshold. Student greeter in place 30 seconds before the bell.
Time budget 5 minutes from the bell.
Success looks like 100% of students seated, materials out, and Do Now started within 5 minutes. Voice level 0-1. Every student greeted by name.

Adult Moves

  • Stand at the threshold and greet each student by name.
  • Use positive presupposition: "Glad you are here. Materials are on your desk."
  • Do not get pulled into correction at the door unless there is an immediate safety issue.
  • Make the Do Now visible before students enter.
  • Circulate during minutes 3-5 to spotlight evidence and award points in real time.

Student Steps

  1. Enter calmly at voice 0-1.
  2. Move directly to your seat.
  3. Put out required materials: notebook, pencil, and the day's task materials.
  4. Start the Do Now within 60 seconds.
  5. Sustain independent work for the full First Five.

Common Slips

  • Students enter loudly or pause to socialize.
  • The teacher starts correcting at the door and loses the flow.
  • The Do Now is unclear or not ready.
  • Materials are missing.
  • Small off-task clusters form before the teacher circulates.

Reteach Move

Stop the routine, re-cue it, and practice entry again for 60 seconds. Restart the timer. Do not skip to consequence language before reteaching the routine. The reteach is the immediate consequence.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Safe: calm entry, voice level held.
  • Respectful: direct response to greeting, smooth entry without disrupting others.
  • Responsible: materials out and Do Now started quickly.

Award points during the routine, not before it. Recognition is for sustained evidence, not the first visible motion.

Leader And Coach Look-Fors

  • The teacher is physically at the threshold.
  • Every student can begin without asking, "What are we doing?"
  • The room settles without heavy narration.
  • The teacher is circulating before minute 5 ends.

Culture Camp Placement

  • Teach entry and greeting on Day 1.
  • Teach First Five expectations on Day 2.
  • Rehearse again after the first weekend and after every long break.
