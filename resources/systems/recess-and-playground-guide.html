Recess and Playground Guide

Audience: Recess duty team, teachers, leaders, culture team

Recess should build belonging and regulation, not suspend them. This routine protects safe play, inclusion, fast recall, and peer repair.

Quick Spec

Element Specification
Cue / signal Class arrives at the recess area and the adult on duty gives the release.
Time budget 20-minute recess block. Recall to line within 60 seconds. Walk back within 90 seconds.
Success looks like Students are engaged in safe play, isolated students are invited into activity, conflicts begin with repair language, and recall happens quickly.

Adult Moves

  • Cover the space with active positions instead of clustering adults together.
  • Stay visible, scanning, and moving.
  • Invite isolated students into activities before exclusion hardens.
  • Coach peer repair instead of taking over every conflict immediately.
  • Use the same recall signal every time.

Student Steps

  1. Stay inside the named boundaries.
  2. Use equipment safely.
  3. Include peers who want to join.
  4. Start conflict repair with regulate first.
  5. Freeze, face, and walk to line at recall.

Common Slips

  • Students are excluded from games.
  • Equipment is misused.
  • Conflict escalates before any repair attempt.
  • Recall is ignored or delayed.

Reteach Move

Choose one recess routine to rebuild at a time. Example: rebuild Recall today and Inclusion tomorrow. Keep reteach specific so students know exactly what is being repaired.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Respectful: inviting another student into play or using peer repair language.
  • Safe: equipment use and immediate response to recall.
  • Responsible: staying in bounds and following through on repair.

Leader And Coach Look-Fors

  • Adults are spread strategically across the space.
  • Students are not left socially isolated without adult notice.
  • Repair language is visible before punishment language.
  • Recall works on the first signal or is immediately rebuilt.

Culture Camp Placement

  • Introduce on Day 7.
  • Practice boundaries, release, inclusion language, and recall separately before a full recess block.
