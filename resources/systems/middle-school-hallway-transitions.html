Middle School Hallway Transitions (Grades 5–8)

Matchbook Learning at Wendell Phillips School 63 • Safe. Respectful. Responsible. • DRAFT October 2026

Audience: Grades 5–8 teachers, restorative team, leaders. This procedure replaces the earlier 6–8 hallway overlay. Grades K–4 continue to use the K–8 Hallway Transitions Guide.

Our Hallway Expectations

Every student, every transition, grades 5–8. Teachers say them inside the room; the restorative team says them in the hall. Same words, every time.

  1. Toes pointed in the direction you're going
  2. Hands to yourself and your space
  3. Voice level 0, silent in the hall
  4. Computer closed, held safely with two hands
  5. Walk on the right, stay on the line
  6. Walk in & start the First Five on the screen

Who Does What

Sending teacher (stays inside, front and center)

  • Says the hallway script before the door opens
  • Checks a full line of computers in hand, looking at every student
  • Releases the line only when everyone is ready
  • Does not walk into the hall

Hallway: restorative team (outside the doors, with radios)

  • Restates the expectations as students come out
  • Watches for voice level 0, the right side, and hands to self
  • Checks for computers and dress code
  • Steps out anyone talking or off-task; keeps the line moving

Receiving teacher (stays inside, front and center)

  • First Five is projected 100% of the time (materials, voice level, exactly what to do, timer)
  • Watches students walk in calmly, no redirection needed
  • Students who don't enter well wait by the door and practice again after everyone is started
  • Gives Skittles to the first students started; checks the seating chart during the First Five

What Everyone Says

Sending teacher (inside): “Stand up and push in your chair. Computer closed, held with two hands. Hold it up so I can see it. Hallway expectations: toes pointed where we're going, hands to yourself, voice level zero. We walk on the right, straight into Room ___, and start the First Five on the screen. Eyes on me, voices off. Let's go.”

Restorative team (hallway): “Voice level zero. Right side. Computer in two hands. Walk straight in and start your First Five.”

Receiving teacher (inside): Points to the screen. Narrates the positive: “I see Room ___ walking in silently and getting started. Thank you.”

Restorative Team: The Redirection Ladder

Start at the top and only move down if you need to. Use the expectation numbers (1–6). Calm voice, close by, short words.

  1. Narrate the positive: “Room 236, I see voice level zero and computers in two hands. Thank you.” Do this first and most often.
  2. Nonverbal cue: finger to lips, point to the right side, or hold up the expectation number.
  3. Quick correction by number: “Number 3: voice level zero.” “Number 5: right side, please.” Say it once, then narrate someone doing it right.
  4. Step out: “Step over here against the wall, please. You're not in trouble. We're going to practice.” The line keeps moving.
  5. Rehearse: “Show me what it looks like: toes pointed, hands to yourself, voice level zero, computer in two hands, right side. Let's try it again.” Up to 10 times, then “That's exactly it. Let's go to class.”
  6. Reset: “We're going to take a reset in the Restorative Room. You'll fill out the form and then get right back to learning.” Calm, never a threat.

Quick fixes:

  • Dress code: “Quick fix before you walk in. Thank you.”
  • No computer: “Let's get it from Room ___ and then straight to class.” (tell the sending teacher afterward)
  • Student argues: “I hear you. Right now, I need number 3. We can talk after.”
  • Student returns: “Welcome back. Fresh start. Let's go.”

Ground rules: Praise more than you correct (3 to 1). Same words, every adult. Small and private. Keep the line moving. Stay calm.

5th Grade (Rooms 205, 207, 209)

No restorative team; teachers run their own transitions. All three rooms are on the same side of the hall. Expectations are exactly the same. Room moves: 205 → ___, 207 → ___, 209 → ___ (to confirm).

  1. Each teacher says the hallway script and checks every computer.
  2. One room moves at a time, on the right side, voice level 0.
  3. Receiving teacher is front and center with the First Five projected.
  4. Students who don't walk in calmly practice again after everyone is started.
  5. Everyone starts the First Five.

6th Grade (Rooms 235, 237, 238)

Room moves: 238 → 235 • 237 → 238 • 235 → 237. Restorative team: 1 member (2 if needed). Goal: no adult needed in the hall.

  1. All three teachers say the hallway script and check every computer.
  2. Room 238 comes out and waits along the right side, voice level 0.
  3. Room 237 walks on the right side directly into 238.
  4. Room 235 comes around and walks straight into 237.
  5. The waiting Room 238 students walk into 235.
  6. Everyone starts the First Five with no redirection.

7th Grade (Rooms 222, 232, 236)

Room moves: 232 → 222 • 236 → 232 • 222 → 236. Restorative team: 3 members with radios, one at each door. The lead handles any rehearsal group.

  1. All three teachers say the hallway script and check every computer.
  2. Room 232 walks to the stairway and waits behind the blue line, leaning against the lockers and wall.
  3. Room 236 walks on the right side directly into 232. No stopping.
  4. The team member from 236 escorts Room 222 into 236.
  5. The team member at 222 sends the waiting Room 232 class into 222.
  6. Everyone starts the First Five with no redirection.

8th Grade (Rooms 231, 233, 234)

Room moves: 234 → 231 • 233 → 234 • 231 → 233. Restorative team: 3 members with radios, one at each door. The lead handles any rehearsal group.

  1. All three teachers say the hallway script and check every computer.
  2. Room 234 walks to the stairway and lines up against the lockers.
  3. Room 233 walks on the right side directly into 234. No stopping.
  4. The team member from 233 escorts Room 231 into 233.
  5. The team member at 231 sends the waiting Room 234 class into 231.
  6. Everyone starts the First Five with no redirection.
