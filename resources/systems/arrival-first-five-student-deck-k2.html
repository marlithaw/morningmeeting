Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

Stand at the door. “Watch how we come in. I will show you first, then you try.” Model the entire entry silently before explaining anything.

Look for

  • Students watch the model all the way through
  • Students pause at the threshold

If it breaks

  • Model again. Young children need to see it more than hear it.

Support adult

  • Stand inside the room so students can see the model from the entry point

Language support

  • Modeling carries this entirely. No language needed.

Visual: Door icon. Stand at the actual threshold while teaching this.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Smell the flower. Blow out the candle. Long and slow. Three times.” Model it with them every single time — do not narrate from the front without doing it.

Look for

  • Exhale longer than inhale
  • Shoulders drop
  • Gesture copied

If it breaks

  • If silliness: model silently once, then invite them back. Do not lecture.

Support adult

  • Breathe audibly beside any student who cannot settle

Language support

  • No language demand — gesture carries it.
  • Same two gestures every day for ten days.

Visual: Flower and candle icons with matching hand gestures.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Four things. Pause. Bag away. Get my work. Sit down. Show me with your body as I say each one.” Walk them through physically.

Look for

  • Students perform each step on cue
  • Order held without prompting by Friday

If it breaks

  • Re-teach one step only.

Support adult

  • Point to the door poster rather than speaking

Language support

  • Four icons, four steps. Post at the door permanently.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. Print this as a floor decal or door poster.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“What comes first? Point to it. Tell your partner.” Then have them tell you the whole order together.

Look for

  • Correct first step identified
  • Students can recite the order chorally

If it breaks

  • Point to the poster and go through it together.

Support adult

  • Hold up the icon cards in order

Language support

  • Pointing is a complete answer.

Visual: Three pictured steps. Students point to the first one.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“Everybody out. We are going to come in the Matchbook way.” Actually do it — leave and re-enter. Repeat three times.

Look for

  • All four steps performed in order
  • It gets faster and quieter each round

If it breaks

  • “Back to the door. One more time, clean.” No commentary on individuals.

Support adult

  • Stand where you can see the whole path

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal — fully accessible.

Visual: Three icons for the rehearsal loop. Actually leave the room and re-enter.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Pick the step that is hardest for you. Point to it. I will look for it the next time you enter class.” Then actually look for it.

Look for

  • Every student points
  • Follow-through at the next class entry

If it breaks

  • Assign one.

Support adult

  • Greet students by name at the door next class and reference their choice

Language support

  • Pointing is sufficient.

Visual: Three pictured promises tied to the four steps.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“How we leave is how we start our learning. Stand. Push in. Turn. Walk. Let's practice.” Do not time this at K–2.

Look for

  • Chairs pushed in unprompted
  • Calm bodies, not rushing

If it breaks

  • “Let's try that again. Back to your spot.” Rebuild clean, no commentary on individuals.

Support adult

  • Stand at the destination, not behind the group

Language support

  • Four icons, four words.
  • No timing pressure — it degrades the routine at this age.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. No stopwatch at this band.
