Hallway Transitions Guide

Audience: Teachers, teacher assistants, leaders, culture team

Hallway travel is a public expression of the culture system. It should look calm, unified, and adult-guided without constant talking. If the hallway routine is strong, students carry order between spaces instead of restarting from zero every time they move.

Quick Spec

Element Specification
Cue / signal Class assembled at the threshold at voice 0 with line leader in front and sweeper at back.
Time budget 90 seconds per 100 feet of travel.
Success looks like Right-side travel, hands at sides, eyes forward, voice 0, line stays cohesive, and arrival happens without gaps or stragglers.

Adult Moves

  • Walk in the back third of the line so the full line stays in view.
  • Use silent cues whenever possible.
  • Avoid verbal correction during hallway travel unless a safety issue requires it.
  • Reinforce fidelity with quick, quiet praise or points after the line holds the routine.
  • Coordinate with other adults in the hall so support is shared.

Student Steps

  1. Travel at voice 0.
  2. Stay on the right side of the hall.
  3. Keep hands at sides and materials carried cleanly.
  4. Maintain line spacing at about arm's length.
  5. Keep eyes forward and do not engage passing groups.

Common Slips

  • Voice creeps above 0.
  • Students stretch the line too far apart.
  • Leaders move too fast or stop abruptly.
  • Students react to other classes in the hall.

Reteach Move

Stop the line, reform it, and rebuild the next 20 feet of travel at voice 0. Then continue. The point is to reteach quickly without turning the reteach into a second disruption.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Safe: voice 0 maintained for the full travel.
  • Respectful: no engagement with passing classes.
  • Responsible: line spacing, clean carry, and strong follow-through to destination.

Leader And Coach Look-Fors

  • Adults are guiding mostly with position and cues, not volume.
  • The back of the line is as strong as the front.
  • The routine survives passing distractions.
  • Arrival looks as controlled as departure.

Culture Camp Placement

  • Introduce on Day 5.
  • Practice the threshold move before full travel.
  • Rebuild after every long break and after any period of visible hallway drift.
