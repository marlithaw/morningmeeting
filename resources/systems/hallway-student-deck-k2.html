Matchbook Culture Camp · Hallway TravelGrades K–2 · 07

Slide 1 · Title & Promise R1, R5

“Other classes are learning while we walk past. The hallway belongs to everybody. Watch how we walk.” Model the whole thing silently.

Look for

  • Students understand other classes are working
  • Students watch the full model

If it breaks

  • Model again, silently.

Support adult

  • Teach this in the actual hallway, not the classroom

Language support

  • Modeling carries it fully.

Visual: Line-up icon. Teach this standing in the actual hallway.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Smell the flower. Blow out the candle. Long and slow. Three times.” Model it with them every single time — do not narrate from the front without doing it.

Look for

  • Exhale longer than inhale
  • Shoulders drop
  • Gesture copied

If it breaks

  • If silliness: model silently once, then invite them back. Do not lecture.

Support adult

  • Breathe audibly beside any student who cannot settle

Language support

  • No language demand — gesture carries it.
  • Same two gestures every day for ten days.

Visual: Flower and candle icons with matching hand gestures.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Voice off. Hands at my sides. Feet walking. Eyes forward. Show me each one.” Call them one at a time, then all four.

Look for

  • Each part produced on cue
  • All four held together for the length of the hallway

If it breaks

  • Re-teach one part only.

Support adult

  • Point to the poster instead of speaking

Language support

  • Four icons, four body parts. Zero language demand.

Visual: Four body-part icons. Print as a hallway wall poster at child height.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“In the hallway, which voice? Point to it.” Then ask about the carpet and the playground so they contrast.

Look for

  • Correct choice for the hallway
  • Students contrast it with other spaces

If it breaks

  • Point to the volume chart.

Support adult

  • Use the same three icons taught on Day 4

Language support

  • Pointing is a complete answer.

Visual: Three volume icons matching the Day 4 lesson exactly.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“Line up. We are going to walk to the end and back. Three times.” Actually walk it. Stop and restart if it breaks.

Look for

  • All four expectations held the whole way
  • Stopping on the hand signal

If it breaks

  • “Back to the start. One more time.” No commentary on individuals.

Support adult

  • Walk at the back so you can see everyone

Language support

  • Physical rehearsal is fully accessible.

Visual: Three icons. Actually walk the hallway — three times.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Pick the one that is hardest for you. Point to it.” Notice it next time you walk.

Look for

  • Every student points
  • Honest self-selection

If it breaks

  • Assign one.

Support adult

  • Name it when you see it in the hallway

Language support

  • Pointing is sufficient.

Visual: Three pictured promises matching the four expectations.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“How we leave is how we start our learning. Stand. Push in. Turn. Walk. Let's practice.” Do not time this at K–2.

Look for

  • Chairs pushed in unprompted
  • Calm bodies, not rushing

If it breaks

  • “Let's try that again. Back to your spot.” Rebuild clean, no commentary on individuals.

Support adult

  • Stand at the destination, not behind the group

Language support

  • Four icons, four words.
  • No timing pressure — it degrades the routine at this age.

Visual: Four numbered step icons. No stopwatch at this band.
