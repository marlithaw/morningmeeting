Procedures Self-Check

A two-minute check on which schoolwide and classroom procedures have been explicitly taught to students, modeled and practiced, not just announced. Any row marked "I want support" can become a 30-minute Procedures Clinic.

How to use it

Mark each procedure honestly

  • Taught and practiced means students saw it modeled and rehearsed it, not only heard the expectation.
  • Students do it without reminders means the routine runs on the cue alone most days.
  • I want support means a coach will run a short clinic on that procedure, then visit for 10 minutes to see it with students.
