A practical operating guide for cafeteria entry, meal service, cleanup, and exit.

Cafeteria Routines Guide

Audience: Teachers, cafeteria duty team, leaders, culture team

The cafeteria should feel structured, safe, and human. Students should be able to eat, talk, and move through the space without chaos. This guide covers entry, the meal period, and exit as one connected routine.

Routine 1: Cafeteria Entry

Element Specification
Cue / signal Class arrives at the cafeteria door and receives entry signal from the adult on duty.
Time budget 3 minutes from arrival at the door to seated or in line.
Success looks like Students enter at voice 0-1, move directly to the lunch line or their seats, and do not cluster at the entrance.

Adult Moves

  • Station an adult at the door.
  • Use a visible handoff from the classroom teacher to the cafeteria adult.
  • Set the voice expectation before students enter.
  • Direct the flow first. Save correction for the reset if the pattern breaks.

Student Steps

  1. Enter at voice 1 or below.
  2. Move directly to the lunch line or assigned seating.
  3. Sit or wait in line without clustering.
  4. Stow belongings in the designated place.

Common Slips

  • Students bunch up at the doorway.
  • Voice spikes on entry.
  • Students cut the line.
  • Bags block walkways.

Reteach Move

Pause the next group at the entrance, ask for the expected voice level and path, then restart the entry.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Safe: voice 1 entry.
  • Responsible: direct movement to seat or line.

Routine 2: Cafeteria While There

Element Specification
Cue / signal Students are seated and the adult cues the meal-start routine.
Time budget 20-minute meal block. Voice 0 reset begins at minute 18.
Success looks like Students remain seated, hold voice 2 or below, clean small spills, dispose of trays in order, and shift to a calm reset before exit.

Adult Moves

  • Keep at least one adult per 40 students visible and circulating.
  • Model the same voice level adults expect from students.
  • Use the silent hand raise as the first whole-group reset move.
  • Award points table by table so the feedback feels predictable and concrete.

Student Steps

  1. Keep voice at 2 or below.
  2. Stay seated unless given permission to move.
  3. Clean small spills with minimal adult dependence.
  4. Dispose of trays only when called.
  5. Shift to voice 0 during the last 2 minutes.

Common Slips

  • Voice rises above 2.
  • Students stand without permission.
  • The dispose station bottlenecks.
  • Food or trash is thrown or left behind.

Reteach Move

Use the silent hand raise, wait for the echo, then name the exact reset needed. Reward the first table that resets the routine cleanly.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Respectful: voice 2 held and peers not disrupted.
  • Responsible: self-cleanup and orderly tray disposal.
  • Safe: staying seated and following the route.

Routine 3: Cafeteria Exit

Element Specification
Cue / signal Silent hand raise at minute 18. Adults dismiss table by table.
Time budget 4 minutes from dismissal cue to line formation. Full reset within 6 minutes.
Success looks like Tables are dismissed in order, the line forms at voice 0, and the space is ready for the next group.

Adult Moves

  • Cue voice 0 before dismissal starts.
  • Name tables showing dismissal-ready posture.
  • Dismiss by table, never by crowd.
  • Complete a clean handoff at the exit back to the classroom teacher or hallway routine.

Student Steps

  1. Shift to voice 0 in the last 2 minutes.
  2. Sit in dismissal-ready posture.
  3. Stand only when the table is called.
  4. Follow the tray route without crossing other groups.
  5. Form the line at the door and wait for the handoff.

Common Slips

  • Students stand before being called.
  • Tray disposal creates collisions.
  • Voice rises during line-up.
  • Trash stays on tables.

Reteach Move

Hold the class briefly at the doorway and name the specific exit move that will be rebuilt the next day. Use visible accountability without humiliation.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Responsible: table reset and orderly disposal.
  • Safe: voice 0 hold through exit.
  • Respectful: clean, patient dismissal.
