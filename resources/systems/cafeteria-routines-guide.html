Cafeteria Routines Guide

Audience: Teachers, cafeteria duty team, leaders, culture team

Lunch runs on the same six steps every day, in every grade band: enter and walk, get your tray, sit at your spot, eat, clean your area, and line up at Level 1. Four adult posts carry the routine. The Greeter welcomes each class at the door. The Navigator keeps the serving line moving and guides each class to its lettered row. The MC runs the countdown on the screen, calls the voice level, and gives the 2-minute signal. The Monitor owns assigned rows, answers raised hands, and checks cleanup. This guide covers entry, the meal, and exit as one connected routine.

Routine 1: Cafeteria Entry

Element Specification
Cue / signal The homeroom teacher walks the class to the cafeteria door, and the Greeter welcomes the class in.
Time budget 3 minutes from arrival at the door to seated at the lettered row.
Success looks like Students walk in at the level on the screen, get everything they need in the serving line, and sit at their spots without clustering.

Adult Moves

  • Greeter stands at the door, greets students by name where possible, and sends the class to the correct serving line.
  • Navigator keeps the line moving and prompts: “Get everything you need before you sit down.”
  • Navigator guides the class to its lettered row (A, B, C…).
  • Direct the flow first. Save correction for the reset if the pattern breaks.

Student Steps

  1. Enter and walk.
  2. Get your tray, utensils, napkin, and drink before sitting down.
  3. Sit at your spot in your class's row.
  4. Stay at your spot.

Common Slips

  • Students bunch up at the doorway.
  • Students sit down without utensils or a drink, then get back up.
  • Students sit outside their row.

Reteach Move

Pause the next class at the door, name the step that broke (“Tray first, then your row”), and restart the entry.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Safe: walking in and through the line.
  • Responsible: everything on the tray before sitting down.

Routine 2: Cafeteria While There

Element Specification
Cue / signal The MC shows the voice level and the countdown on the screen.
Time budget The full meal block, with the MC's countdown on the screen the whole time.
Success looks like Students stay at their spots, match the level on the screen, keep food on the tray, and raise a hand when they need something.

Adult Moves

  • MC shows the level on the screen and calls every change. Level 3, table voice, is the highest in the cafeteria.
  • Monitor stays with assigned rows, scans continuously, and comes to any raised hand.
  • Monitor watches the first two minutes for any student with nowhere to sit and helps that student find a spot without announcing it.
  • Name what is going right out loud, by row and by value: “Row C held the level the whole period. Respectful point.”
  • Give a direction once, restate it once in the same words, and use the Reflect and Repair table when it still does not happen.

Student Steps

  1. Match the level on the screen.
  2. Stay at your spot. Need something? Raise your hand.
  3. Keep food on the tray.
  4. Make room when someone needs a seat.

Common Slips

  • Voice rises above the level on the screen.
  • Students get up to find an adult or a friend.
  • Food is traded between rows or thrown.

Reteach Move

Point to the screen, name the level once, and wait. Recognize the first row that matches it.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Respectful: matching the level and making room.
  • Responsible: raising a hand and staying at the spot.
  • Safe: food stays on the tray.

Routine 3: Cafeteria Exit

Element Specification
Cue / signal The MC gives the same 2-minute signal every day.
Time budget 2 minutes to clean. Rows are then released one at a time.
Success looks like Every area is clean and sorted, rows line up at Level 1 when called, and the space is ready for the next group.

Adult Moves

  • Monitor checks each row before release. A row that is not ready fixes it before leaving.
  • Release one row at a time, never the whole room: “Row A, sorted and clear. Line up.”
  • The homeroom teacher receives the class at the line. The Hallway Transitions routine applies from the cafeteria door.

Student Steps

  1. At the 2-minute signal, clean your area and sort your trash.
  2. Wait at your spot until your row is called.
  3. Walk to the line at Level 1 and face forward.

Common Slips

  • Students stand before their row is called.
  • Trash stays at the spot.
  • Voice rises during line-up.

Reteach Move

Hold the row, name the step that broke (“Sorted and clear first”), and release the row once it is ready.

LiveSchool Point Hooks

  • Responsible: a clean, sorted area.
  • Safe: walking to the line when called.
  • Respectful: Level 1 in line.

Reflect and Repair

When a direction has been given twice and not followed, or when something causes harm to a person, property, or the room, the student goes to the Reflect and Repair table with the grade-band sheet. The student keeps eating. There are two parts: the student writes about what happened, then does the repair they pick. An adult checks both and initials the sheet. Names of students who do not finish go on the Reflect and Repair list, which goes to a Co-Dean at the end of every lunch period. The full steps are on the Lunch Expectations procedure card.
