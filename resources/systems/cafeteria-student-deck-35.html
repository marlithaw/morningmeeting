Slide 1 · Title & Promise

“Lunch runs the same way every single day. When everyone knows the next step, lunch is calm and you get more time to eat and talk.”

Look for

  • Students can say lunch has a set routine

If it breaks

  • Point to the six-step poster in the cafeteria.

Support adult

  • Post the six-step strip where students line up.

Language support

  • Point and name each picture.

Slide 2 · Regulate

Count the breaths aloud with students. Three rounds.

Look for

  • Exhale visibly longer
  • Shoulders drop by round two

If it breaks

  • “This one is for you. One more round.”

Support adult

  • Co-regulate beside students who struggle.

Language support

  • Model rather than describe.

Slide 3 · The Six Steps

Walk through all six steps and act each one out: enter and walk, get your tray, sit at your spot, eat, clean your area, line up at Level 1.

Look for

  • Students can name the six steps in order
  • Students say why the tray comes before the seat

If it breaks

  • “Which step comes next?” Point to the poster.
  • Rehearse the step that was missed, not the whole routine.

Support adult

  • Stand where students can see you model each step.

Language support

  • Gesture each step while naming it.
  • enter · entrar, tray · bandeja

Slide 4 · Lettered Rows

“Every class has a lettered row. You sit at your spot in your row and you stay there. If you need something, your hand goes up and an adult comes to you.”

Look for

  • Students know their row letter
  • Students say what to do instead of getting up

If it breaks

  • Practice the hand-up signal once.

Support adult

  • Tell students their row letter before the first practice.

Language support

  • Show the letter on the board.
  • row · fila

Slide 5 · Voice Levels

“Lunch has four levels you can use: 0, 1, 2, and 3. Level 3 is table voice, the loudest we go. Level 4 is for outside only. The MC shows the level on the screen.”

Look for

  • Students can demonstrate levels 1, 2, and 3
  • Students name Level 1 for lining up

If it breaks

  • Practice each level for five seconds, then stop.

Support adult

  • Use the same level words the MC uses.

Language support

  • Show the number with fingers while saying it.

Slide 6 · Adult Posts

“There are four adult jobs at lunch. The Greeter meets you at the door. The Navigator keeps the line moving and helps you find your row. The MC runs the countdown and calls the voice level. The Monitor watches your row and helps with cleanup.”

Look for

  • Students can say which adult helps with what

If it breaks

  • “Who would you look for if you spilled?” (Monitor)

Support adult

  • If possible, introduce the actual adults who hold each post.

Language support

  • Point to each role card while naming the job.

Slide 7 · Countdown, Cleanup, Release

“The timer on the screen tells you how much time is left. At the 2-minute signal, you clean your area. Rows leave one at a time, and only when your row is called.”

Look for

  • Students name the 2-minute signal
  • Students wait for their row to be called

If it breaks

  • Run the release once: one row stands, the others stay seated.

Support adult

  • Stand at the line spot to model Level 1.

Language support

  • clean · limpiar

Slide 8 · Reflect and Repair Table

“If an adult gives a direction two times and it does not happen, or if something causes harm, you go to the Reflect and Repair table. You keep eating. There are two parts: you write about what happened, then you do the repair you pick. Then you are done. It is how you make it right, not a punishment.”

Look for

  • Students describe the table as a way to make it right, not a punishment
  • Students know they keep eating

If it breaks

  • “What are the two parts?” (Write it. Do the repair.)

Support adult

  • Keep the tone calm and matter-of-fact.

Language support

  • Act out each step with a gesture.
  • repair · reparar

Slide 9 · Make Room

“Part of lunch is noticing who has nowhere to sit and making room before they have to ask.”

Look for

  • Students name one way to invite someone

If it breaks

  • “Have you ever had to ask? How did that feel?”

Support adult

  • Watch the first two minutes. That is when exclusion happens.

Language support

  • Pair newly arrived students deliberately.

Slide 10 · Commitment

Have each student say their commitment to a partner, then call on two or three.

Look for

  • Every student states one step they will own

If it breaks

  • Offer the word bank aloud.

Support adult

  • Revisit the commitments after the first real lunch.

Language support

  • Accept a pointed or one-word answer.
