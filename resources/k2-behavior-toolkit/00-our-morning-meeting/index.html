The Our Morning Meeting song

How to use the song

  • This is the Relax song from Weekly Videos, set up for the projector. It runs about 2 minutes 12 seconds.
  • The six parts light up on the right as the song names them. Students do each move when its part lights up.
  • The deck plays it from slide 1, and slide 5 plays one part at a time when you tap a picture.
  • Keep playing it every morning in the Regulate phase. Watch for the moves and name them.
