Matchbook Morning Meeting · Expectation PracticeGrades 6–8 · 07

Slide 1 · Launch R1, R5

“You already know the expectations. Reputation comes from reps, not reminders. Today we practice on purpose.”

Look for

  • Students engage as skill, not compliance
  • Low defensiveness

If it breaks

  • Name the ‘why’: pressure reveals practice.

Support adult

  • Keep tone respectful and adult.

Language support

  • Anchor phrase: run the reps.

Visual: Target icon.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Two rounds of box breathing. In four, hold four, out four, hold four. Regulation is a skill, not a mood.”

Look for

  • Slower, even breathing
  • Settled posture

If it breaks

  • Offer feet-down anchor instead.

Support adult

  • Model without forcing eyes closed.

Language support

  • Visual box guides the count.

Visual: Box + anchor icons.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Define each expectation as an action in this room. Safe, Respectful, Responsible — what does each look like at 8th-grade level?”

Look for

  • Actions, not slogans
  • All three defined

If it breaks

  • Sharpen the vaguest one together.

Support adult

  • Reference the shared standard.

Language support

  • Icons cue each expectation.

Visual: Three value icons.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“Name the setting where you actually break this. Privately is fine. Honesty is the rep.”

Look for

  • Honest, specific growth edge
  • Real settings named

If it breaks

  • Let them write it privately.

Support adult

  • Normalize that everyone has an edge.

Language support

  • Frame + word bank scaffold it.

Visual: Setting word bank.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“Model it, rehearse it, refine it, own it. We practice the move before we need it.”

Look for

  • Genuine rehearsal, not performance
  • Second rep is cleaner

If it breaks

  • Reset and re-model the move.

Support adult

  • Coach the refine step specifically.

Language support

  • Peer rehearsal lowers stakes.

Visual: Model, rehearse, refine, own.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Write one rep: setting, action, purpose. Observable and specific — camera-confirmable.”

Look for

  • Observable, purposeful commitments
  • Setting + action + why

If it breaks

  • Give the stem and one model.

Support adult

  • Follow up privately later.

Language support

  • Private writing supports candor.

Visual: Commitment frame.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“Name it, notice it, do it, repair when you miss. Every space is another rep — including your phone.”

Look for

  • Transfer to real settings
  • Ownership of repair

If it breaks

  • Re-anchor to the three words.

Support adult

  • Reference commitments through the day.

Language support

  • Four cues, four words.

Visual: Four icons across settings.
