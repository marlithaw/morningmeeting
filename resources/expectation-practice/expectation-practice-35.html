Matchbook Morning Meeting · Expectation PracticeGrades 3–5 · 07

Slide 1 · Launch R1, R5

“Expectations aren’t just posted — they’re practiced. Today we run a rep so ours hold up under pressure.”

Look for

  • Students treat this as skill-building
  • Engaged, not defensive

If it breaks

  • Reframe: practice, not punishment.

Support adult

  • Name the purpose in one line.

Language support

  • Anchor word: rehearse.

Visual: Target icon.

Slide 2 · Regulate R1

“Three rounds: in through the flower, out through the candle. Settle your body first.”

Look for

  • Longer exhale than inhale
  • Bodies still

If it breaks

  • Model one silent round.

Support adult

  • Breathe with the group.

Language support

  • Gesture models the pace.

Visual: Flower and candle icons.

Slide 3 · Orient R1, R5

“Safe, Respectful, Responsible. Give me one specific example of each — an action, not a feeling.”

Look for

  • Concrete examples for all three
  • Actions, not adjectives

If it breaks

  • Re-teach the fuzzy one.

Support adult

  • Point to the room anchor.

Language support

  • Icons cue each word.

Visual: Three value icons matching your room poster.

Slide 4 · Connect R2

“Pick the setting where this is genuinely hard for you. Honesty makes the rep worth it.”

Look for

  • Real, specific settings
  • Honest self-selection

If it breaks

  • Offer the list of settings aloud.

Support adult

  • Normalize that everyone has a hard setting.

Language support

  • Sentence frame + word bank.

Visual: Setting word bank.

Slide 5 · Practice R4, R5

“Watch the model, rehearse it with a partner, adjust, and repeat until it’s clean.”

Look for

  • Partners rehearse the real move
  • Feedback improves the second try

If it breaks

  • Re-model and reset the rep.

Support adult

  • Circulate and coach the adjust step.

Language support

  • Partner talk lowers the stakes.

Visual: See, do, adjust, lock.

Slide 6 · Commit R2, R5

“Finish the sentence with a setting and an observable action. Keep it small enough to actually do.”

Look for

  • Observable, specific commitments
  • Setting named

If it breaks

  • Give a sentence stem and one example.

Support adult

  • Catch and name the action later today.

Language support

  • Written or spoken both count.

Visual: Commitment frame.

Slide 7 · Transition R1, R4, R5

“Name it, watch for it, do it, own it. Every space today is another rep.”

Look for

  • Students connect to other settings
  • Calm, purposeful exit

If it breaks

  • Re-anchor with the three words.

Support adult

  • Reference the commitment during the day.

Language support

  • Four cues, four words.

Visual: Four icons across routines.
