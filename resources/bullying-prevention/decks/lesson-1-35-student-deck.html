Power + Pattern + Harm

01

Power

A real or perceived advantage—strength, popularity, information, identity, group size, or access.

02

Pattern

Repeated behavior or behavior likely to be repeated.

03

Harm

Unwanted behavior that hurts, scares, humiliates, intimidates, or excludes.
