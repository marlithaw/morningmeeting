Matchbook Morning Meeting · Upstander, Not Bystander3–5 · 03
Activity · Learn the moveRespectful + Responsible

The 3S upstander plan

01

Speak or redirect

If safe: “That’s not okay.” Change the subject, interrupt the audience, or invite the person away.

02

Support

Check in privately, sit beside the person, include them, and avoid blaming.

03

Send for help

Report to a trusted adult. For danger, get help immediately instead of stepping in.
