Matchbook Morning Meeting · Upstander, Not BystanderK–2 · 03
Activity · Learn the moveRespectful + Responsible

Three safe upstander moves

01

Stop

If it feels safe, use a clear voice: “Stop. That is not okay.”

02

Support

Stand with the person, invite them with you, or say something kind.

03

Tell

Get a trusted adult right away. Keep telling until someone helps.
