Thankful Together: Gratitude We Can See and Hear

This week teaches gratitude as a skill students practice, not a feeling they are told to have. Gratitude starts with noticing the good and the people behind it. A strong thank-you names what someone did and how it helped. Gratitude also shows up in actions: helping, taking care of shared things, and including others. Thursday, students thank the people who keep our school running. Every share is a choice, and every student’s gratitude counts, big or small.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Full week: Morning Meeting runs Monday through Thursday, November 16–19.

Thursday: students write a thank-you note to a school helper, named by role (for example, the cafeteria team, a bus driver, the custodians, the front office). Students may hand their note to that helper during the day.

Friday, November 20: Friday Wonder runs as Friday’s Morning Spark on giving credit and shout-outs, followed by Friday Cypher.
