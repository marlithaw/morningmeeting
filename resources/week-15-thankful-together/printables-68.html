Tuesday November 17 · Grades 6–8

Say It Like You Mean It

Name: _____________________

Students: Rate three thank-yous from weak to sincere. Draft a thank-you to someone who helped you.

Estudiantes: Califica tres agradecimientos de débil a sincero. Escribe un borrador de agradecimiento para alguien que te ayudó.

Rank 1 (weak) to 3 (sincere): “thx” · “Thanks for helping me.” · “Thank you for staying late; I made both free throws, and it meant a lot.”

My draft thank-you (action + impact + what it meant):

Take home: Say It Out Loud
Family chat: Who has made a difference for you that you have never told?
