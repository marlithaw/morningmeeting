Week hubTeacher guide

Respect You Can See. Responsibility You Can Count On.

This week turns belonging into everyday action. Students practice what respect looks and sounds like, how to own a mistake and repair it, and how to keep the same standard in every space at school, with every adult, and when no one is watching.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Short week: no school for students on Tuesday, November 3. Morning Meeting runs Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

Friday, November 6: Friday Wonder runs as Friday’s Morning Spark on the same theme, followed by Friday Cypher.
