Monday November 2 · Grades 6–8

Respond, Don’t React

Name: _____________________

Students: Rewrite two reactions as respectful responses. Name what your tone and body will show.

Estudiantes: Reescribe dos reacciones como respuestas respetuosas. Nombra lo que mostrarán tu tono y tu cuerpo.

“Whatever.” → My respectful response:
“That makes no sense.” → My respectful response:
My tone will be… My body will show…

My commitment: When I disagree this week, I will say… My tone and body will show…
Cuando no esté de acuerdo esta semana, diré… Mi tono y mi cuerpo mostrarán…

Take home: Respect Under Pressure
Family chat: When is it hardest to stay respectful, and what helps?
Charla en familia: ¿Cuándo es más difícil mantener el respeto y qué ayuda?

Point, say, draw, dictate, or write.

Wednesday November 4 · Grades 6–8

Intent, Impact, Repair

Name: _____________________

Students: Sort each response: excuse or ownership. Write a repair plan with one action.

Estudiantes: Clasifica cada respuesta: excusa o reconocimiento. Escribe un plan de reparación con una acción.

  1. E · O   “They’re too sensitive.”
  2. E · O   “I didn’t think about how that would land. I’m sorry.”
  3. E · O   “Everybody else was doing it.”
  4. E · O   “I’ll delete it and talk to them.”
Excuse or ownership? Label each response on the slide: E or O.
The impact was…
My repair action:

My commitment: When my impact does not match my intent, I will… One repair action I can take is…
Cuando mi impacto no coincida con mi intención, voy a… Una acción de reparación que puedo hacer es…

Take home: Repair Conversation
Family chat: What does a real repair look like in our family?
Charla en familia: ¿Cómo se ve una reparación real en nuestra familia?

Point, say, draw, dictate, or write.
