Wednesday · Welcome · Ready screen + video

Run the room’s usual greeting.

Select Watch the First 5 on this slide to play the Relax · First 5 video. Before it starts: “This is what the First Five looks like when it is working. Watch how they keep going.” After it ends, take one answer.

“When a bike ride gets hard, what happens if you stop pedaling? You roll back. Keep going is how you get up the hill.”

Look for

  • Students connect the picture to work
  • Students name one thing they saw in the video

If it breaks

  • Keep the hook to one minute.

Support adult

  • Name yesterday’s live rep: who started first.

Language support

  • The picture carries the idea.

Visual: Bike icon on red.

Wednesday · Regulate · 1:00

“Smell the flower. Blow the candle. Long and slow. Three times.” Breathe with students every time.

Look for

  • Breath out is longer than breath in
  • Shoulders drop

If it breaks

  • If it gets silly, model it once in silence and start again.

Support adult

  • Breathe beside any student who needs help settling.

Language support

  • The gesture carries the meaning.

Visual: Flower and candle icons.

Wednesday · Orient · 2:00

“Keep going means you work until the timer ends, with your voice off. If something is hard, you try your best and keep going. You do not raise your hand. I come to you.”

Advance to the When I’m Done and When I Need Something slides.

Look for

  • Students can say what stuck means

If it breaks

  • Ask one student to say the stuck rule back.

Support adult

  • Stuck has the same answer in every room.

Language support

  • Three icons, three rules.

Visual: Timer, voice off, and stuck icons.

Wednesday · Practice · 6:00

Choose the modelChoose a student who has struggled to keep working, at a moment they are likely to succeed. Tell them the plan quietly before the lesson: “When you get to a hard part, try your best and keep going. When you finish, do the done task.”

Optional non-example (teacher plays it)Start working, hit a hard part, put the pencil down, raise a hand, look around. “What happened to my work?” Take two answers. Then: “Now watch [Name].”

Run itThe model works on the practice Do Now for about 30 seconds while the class watches.

Narrate: “[Name] hit a hard part. [Name] tried, and kept going. Voice off. Still working.”

Ask the class“What did [Name] do when it got hard?” “What will [Name] do when done?”

If it is excellent

“Did you see that? [Name] did every step. That is what excellent looks like. Now it is time for all of us to show how excellent we can be.”

If it is partly right

“[Name] got some steps just right. Who saw the step to fix?” Take one answer. “[Name], one more time with that fix.” Then: “Now all of us.”

If it turns silly

Calm, one line, no audience: “Thank you. That is exactly why we practice.” Offer one redo: “[Name], show us the real one.” If the student says no or keeps playing: “Thanks. [Next student], show us.” Keep moving and follow up privately later with the usual correction steps. Then: “That is why we practice here first. Then we do it for real.”

Then everyoneFull routine, then 2 minutes of keep going on the practice Do Now. Narrate by name, choosing students who have not been named yet this week.

If the class rep breaks“Let’s get that one more time so it is easy tomorrow.” One redo. If it still breaks, practice only the one step that broke.

Look for

  • The model keeps working through a hard part
  • Most students still working when the timer ends

Support adult

  • Walk the room during the class rep; do not help, just narrate.

Language support

  • Drawings and lists count as work.

Visual: Watch, name it, everyone keeps going.

Wednesday · Commit · 2:00

“When you keep going, you are being Responsible, and you can earn a point.” Give three points out loud, naming the move each time: “Amara started without being asked. That is a Responsible point.”

“When everyone keeps going until the timer ends, our class earns a spot on the Brag Board.”

“Say it with me: I keep going.”

Look for

  • Points go to three different students, each for a named move

If it breaks

  • Name the move before the point, every time.

Support adult

  • Have LiveSchool open before the lesson.

Language support

  • Two icons: one for me, one for all of us.

Visual: LiveSchool badge and Brag Board icons.
