Next Time, It’s Live

The real rep is the next time we come in or move to our seats.

Matchbook Morning Meeting · The First FiveGrades 1–2 · Monday

Monday · Welcome · Ready screen + video

Run the room’s usual greeting.

Select Watch the First 5 on this slide to play the Relax · First 5 video. Before it starts: “This is what the First Five looks like when it is working. Watch how they come in and get ready.” After it ends, take one answer.

“Before a game starts, does the team stand around and ask where to go? No. Every player knows their spot. Today we learn how we come into our room so everyone knows their spot.”

Look for

  • Students connect the team to the class
  • Students name one thing they saw in the video

If it breaks

  • Keep the hook to one minute.

Support adult

  • Invite a student who plays a sport to show where they line up.

Language support

  • The basketball picture carries the idea.

Visual: Basketball icon on red.

Monday · Regulate · 1:00

“Smell the flower. Blow the candle. Long and slow. Three times.” Breathe with students every time.

Look for

  • Breath out is longer than breath in
  • Shoulders drop

If it breaks

  • If it gets silly, model it once in silence and start again.

Support adult

  • Breathe beside any student who needs help settling.

Language support

  • The gesture carries the meaning.

Visual: Flower and candle icons.

Monday · Orient · 2:00

Teach each word with its motion. Students echo each one.

“Some rooms pick up materials at the door. Some rooms have materials waiting at the seat. Both are right. Here is how our room does it.” Advance to the Our Room slide.

Look for

  • Students say and show each step
  • Every student participates

If it breaks

  • Repeat the three steps slower, with the motions.

Support adult

  • Point to the icons as each step is named.

Language support

  • Every step has an icon and a hand motion; showing counts as answering.

Visual: Three numbered step icons: door, chair, materials.

Monday · Orient · 2:00 · Option A: Grab and Go

“In our room, we grab and go. Pick up your materials at the door, then go straight to your seat.”

Look for

  • Students can say the order back

If it breaks

  • Have two students show the order with motions.

Support adult

  • Stand at the door and point to where materials will be.

Language support

  • The order of the icons shows the order of the steps.

Visual: Door, materials, chair, in that order.

Monday · Orient · 2:00 · Option B: Ready at Your Seat

“In our room, your materials are ready at your seat. Go straight to your seat and take them out.”

Look for

  • Students can say the order back

If it breaks

  • Have two students show the order with motions.

Support adult

  • Point to a seat with materials set out.

Language support

  • The order of the icons shows the order of the steps.

Visual: Door, chair, materials, in that order.

Monday · Connect · 2:00

“Tell your partner: when you come into our room, what do you do first?” Take two answers and match them to the steps.

Look for

  • Answers match the room’s path

If it breaks

  • Point back to the Our Room slide.

Support adult

  • Pair new students with a steady partner.

Language support

  • Pointing to a step is a full answer.

Visual: Three step icons as answer choices.

Monday · Practice · 6:00

Choose the modelBefore the lesson, quietly ask one student who usually comes in smoothly: “I am going to ask you to show the class how we come in. Ready?”

Optional non-example (teacher plays it)“Watch me come in. Find what is wrong.” Walk in slowly, wave and chat, wander past the seat, forget materials. “What did you see?” Take two answers. Then: “Now watch [Name] do it the right way.”

Run itThe model steps to the edge of the carpet or the door and comes in following this room’s path.

Narrate: “[Name] is coming in. Walking feet. Voice off. Straight to the seat. Things out. Hands still.”

Ask the class“What did [Name]‘s feet do?” “What did [Name]‘s voice do?” “What did [Name] do first?”

If it is excellent

“Did you see that? [Name] did every step. That is what excellent looks like. Now it is time for all of us to show how excellent we can be.”

If it is partly right

“[Name] got some steps just right. Who saw the step to fix?” Take one answer. “[Name], one more time with that fix.” Then: “Now all of us.”

If it turns silly

Calm, one line, no audience: “Thank you. That is exactly why we practice.” Offer one redo: “[Name], show us the real one.” If the student says no or keeps playing: “Thanks. [Next student], show us.” Keep moving and follow up privately later with the usual correction steps. Then: “That is why we practice here first. Then we do it for real.”

Then everyoneHalf the class steps into the hallway and comes back in. The other half watches and shows the motion for each step they see. Switch. Narrate by name: “Darius went straight to his seat. That is Responsible.”

If the class rep breaks“Let’s get that one more time so it is easy tomorrow.” One redo. If it still breaks, practice only the one step that broke.

Look for

  • The model does every step
  • The class names the steps in order
  • Every student completes a rep

Support adult

  • Stand at the door during the class rep so the threshold is covered.

Language support

  • Watching a classmate shows the routine without words.
  • Pointing and showing count as full participation.

Visual: Three steps: watch, name it, show me.

Monday · Commit · 2:00

“Did your group come in, sit down, and get ready? Show me your thumb.” Name one thing that went right.

“Say it with me: I know my spot. I know my steps.” Students echo.

Look for

  • Honest thumbs
  • Every voice in the echo

If it breaks

  • Echo it a second time, softer.

Support adult

  • Catch it later today and name it.

Language support

  • The echo gives every student the words.

Visual: The three step icons with the promise line.

Monday · Transition · 2:00

“The next time we come in, that is the real one. I will be watching for every step, and I will name who does it.”

Then, at the first transition after Morning Meeting, name the steps out loud as students do them.

Look for

  • Students know when the live rep is

If it breaks

  • Point to the door and say when.

Support adult

  • At the live rep, narrate at least two students by name.

Language support

  • The door icon marks the live rep.

Visual: Door icon on red.
