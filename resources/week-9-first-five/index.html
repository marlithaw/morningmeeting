One predictable meeting routine

Regulate 1 · Orient 2 · Connect 2 · Practice 6 · Commit 2 · Transition 2 = 15 minutes

Welcome is a ready screen with the Relax · First 5 video. Each lesson asks once how the room runs (materials path, Do Now location, done task, supply routine, and for K–4 the mark system) and then shows only the matching slides. In Practice, a classmate models the step, the class names what they saw, and everyone rehearses. Friday Cypher remains separate.
