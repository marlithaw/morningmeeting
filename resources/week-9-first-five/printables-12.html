Every day · Grades 1–2 · Teacher companion

Watch · Name · Show

Choose the model

Monday: a student who usually comes in smoothly. Tuesday: a different student. Wednesday: a student who has struggled to keep working, at a moment they are likely to succeed. Thursday: a student who has not modeled yet. Ask quietly before the lesson. Never ask a student to model the wrong way; the teacher plays any non-example.

Narrate

Name the student, the action, and the value while it happens.

If it is excellent

“Did you see that? [Name] did every step. That is what excellent looks like. Now it is time for all of us to show how excellent we can be.”

If it is partly right

“[Name] got some steps just right. Who saw the step to fix?” Take one answer. “[Name], one more time with that fix.” Then: “Now all of us.”

If it turns silly

Calm, one line, no audience: “Thank you. That is exactly why we practice.” Offer one redo: “[Name], show us the real one.” If the student says no or keeps playing: “Thanks. [Next student], show us.” Keep moving and follow up privately later with the usual correction steps. Then: “That is why we practice here first. Then we do it for real.”

Live rep
