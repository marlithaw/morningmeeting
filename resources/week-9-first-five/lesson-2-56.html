Matchbook Morning Meeting · The First FiveGrades 5–6 · Tuesday

Tuesday · Welcome · Ready screen + video

Run the room’s usual greeting.

Select Watch the First 5 on this slide to play the Relax · First 5 video. Before it starts: “This is what the First Five looks like when it is working. Watch how fast they start.” After it ends, take one answer.

“Athletes warm up before they play. Musicians warm up before they perform. The Do Now is your brain’s warm-up. It is easy on purpose, and it is not graded like a test.”

Look for

  • Students are listening, not moving
  • Students name one thing they saw in the video

If it breaks

  • Keep the hook to one minute.

Support adult

  • Name yesterday’s live rep: who did it well.

Language support

  • The picture carries the comparison.

Visual: Runner icon on red.

Tuesday · Regulate · 1:00

“In for four, out for six. The breath out is the one that calms you. Three rounds.” Count aloud with students.

Look for

  • Breath out is longer than breath in
  • Room is quiet by the third round

If it breaks

  • Restart the count once. Keep it calm and short.

Support adult

  • Count with fingers for students who need a visual.

Language support

  • Fingers show the count.

Visual: Lungs and candle icons.

Tuesday · Orient · 2:00

“Do Now in 60 means you start within 60 seconds of sitting down, without anyone telling you. In every class, the Do Now is ready before you walk in, in the same place every day.” Advance to the Our Room slide.

Look for

  • Students can say what Start means

If it breaks

  • Model sit, look, start in five seconds.

Support adult

  • Point to where the Do Now will be.

Language support

  • Sit, look, start: three icons, three actions.

Visual: Sit, look, start icons.

Tuesday · Orient · 2:00 · Option A: On the Board

“In this room, the Do Now is always on the board when you walk in. Sit, look at the board, start.”

Look for

  • Students look to the board

If it breaks

  • Point to the board and say it again.

Support adult

  • Post today’s practice Do Now on the board as the example.

Language support

  • The board icon shows where to look.

Visual: Board icon.

Tuesday · Orient · 2:00 · Option B: On Your Desk

“In this room, the Do Now is always on the paper at your seat. Sit, look at your paper, start.”

Look for

  • Students look to their desk

If it breaks

  • Hold up a paper and say it again.

Support adult

  • Set a paper on one desk as the example.

Language support

  • The paper icon shows where to look.

Visual: Paper icon.

Tuesday · Connect · 2:00

“Tell your partner: what slows a start down?” Take two honest answers: looking for materials, talking, not seeing the Do Now.

Look for

  • Every student turns to a partner

If it breaks

  • Give your own example first.

Support adult

  • Sit with a pair that needs a partner.

Language support

  • Sentence stem on screen; pointing counts.

Visual: Turn-and-talk frame with a sentence stem.

Tuesday · Practice · 6:00

Choose the modelChoose a different student from Monday. Ask quietly before the lesson.

Optional non-example (teacher plays it)Sit down, look around, ask a neighbor what to do, wait. “What slowed me down?” Take two answers. Then: “Now watch [Name].”

Run itThe model comes in, sits, looks, and starts the practice Do Now on the slide. A second student runs the stopwatch.

Narrate: “[Name] is seated. Eyes on the Do Now. Pencil moving. No one had to tell [Name].”

Ask the class“How many seconds from sitting to starting?” “Did [Name] ask anyone what to do?”

If it is excellent

“That is the standard. Every step, no reminders. Now it is time for all of us to show how excellent we can be.”

If it is partly right

“Most of that was exactly right. Who saw what to adjust?” Take one answer. “Run it again with that fix.” Then: “Now all of us.”

If it turns silly

Calm, one line, no audience: “Thank you. That is exactly why we rehearse.” Offer one redo: “Show us the real one.” If the student declines or keeps performing: “Thanks. [Next student], you are up.” Keep moving and follow up privately later with the usual correction steps. Then: “We rehearse it here so we can take it live.”

Then everyoneFull routine for everyone: enter, seat, materials, start. Use paper and pencils and a stopwatch, started when the first student sits. Name the first three who start: “Jada, Omar, and Lin started right away.” Then: “Hands up if you started in 60.” Count honestly.

If the class rep breaks“Let’s get that one more time so it is easy tomorrow.” One redo. If it still breaks, practice only the one step that broke.

Look for

  • The model starts without a prompt
  • Most students start within the time

Support adult

  • Have the practice Do Now ready before the rep.

Language support

  • The practice Do Now can be answered with a drawing or a list.

Visual: Watch, name it, everyone starts.

Tuesday · Commit · 2:00

“Started in 60? Show me: thumbs up, or not yet.” Name what worked.

“Say it with me: Sit. Look. Start.”

Look for

  • Honest self-ratings

If it breaks

  • Echo it again, softer.

Support adult

  • Name the students who started first at the live rep.

Language support

  • The echo gives every student the words.

Visual: Sit, look, start icons with the promise.

Tuesday · Transition · 2:00

“The next time you sit down, the Do Now is the real one. Sit, look, start.”

At the live rep, name the first three students who start.

Look for

  • Students know when the live rep is

If it breaks

  • Say when the next Do Now is.

Support adult

  • Have the next Do Now posted before students arrive.

Language support

  • The icon matches the step.

Visual: Start icon on red.
