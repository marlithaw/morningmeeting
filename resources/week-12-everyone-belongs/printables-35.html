Monday October 26 · Grades 3–5

Welcome Comic

Use the Welcome Comic worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 7.

Students: Create a two-panel welcome comic. Add an invitation and a meaningful way to join.

Estudiantes: Crea una historieta de bienvenida de dos cuadros. Agrega una invitación y una forma significativa de participar.

Take home: Welcome Challenge
Family chat: What makes an invitation feel welcoming?
Charla en familia: ¿Qué hace que una invitación se sienta acogedora?

Commitment frame: My invitation would be… A meaningful role I could offer is…
Mi invitación sería… Un papel importante que podría ofrecer es…

Answer notes: A strong answer pairs an invitation (“Want to join us?”) with a real job, such as recorder, materials manager, or idea checker, and lets the classmate choose.

Tuesday October 27 · Grades 3–5

Fact or Myth

Use the Fact or Myth worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 8.

Students: Choose fact or myth for each statement. Explain one answer to a partner.

Estudiantes: Elige hecho o mito para cada oración. Explica una respuesta a un compañero.

Take home: Not Always Visible
Family chat: Why should we avoid guessing someone’s needs from appearance?
Charla en familia: ¿Por qué no debemos adivinar las necesidades de alguien por su apariencia?

Commitment frame: I can show patience by…
Puedo mostrar paciencia…

Answer notes: Myth: You can always see a disability. Fact: People may need extra time to respond. Fact: Ask before helping and respect the answer.

Wednesday October 28 · Grades 3–5

Words That Help

Use the Words That Help worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 9.

Students: Rewrite the comment as a respectful invitation. Choose two safe next steps.

Estudiantes: Reescribe el comentario como una invitación respetuosa. Elige dos pasos seguros.

Take home: Words That Help Card
Family chat: What can you do if you hear a hurtful comment?
Charla en familia: ¿Qué puedes hacer si escuchas un comentario hiriente?

Commitment frame: A safe response is… An adult who can help is…
Una respuesta segura es… Un adulto que puede ayudar es…

Answer notes: A respectful rewrite: “Come join us. You can share your idea your way.” Safe next steps: tell a trusted adult and check in with the peer. Joining in to fit in, arguing, or threatening are not safe.

Thursday October 29 · Grades 3–5

Access Detective

Use the Access Detective worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 10.

Students: Sketch or describe a way to answer using pictures. Explain how the change helps.

Estudiantes: Dibuja o describe una forma de responder con imágenes. Explica cómo ayuda el cambio.

Take home: Home Access Detective
Family chat: How can different tools help people do the same activity?
Charla en familia: ¿Cómo pueden diferentes herramientas ayudar a las personas a hacer la misma actividad?

Commitment frame: One change is… It helps because…
Un cambio es… Ayuda porque…

Answer notes: Accept drawing, pointing, a communication board, answer cards, or another workable response option. The change lets the student show the same thinking a different way.
