Monday October 26 · Grades K–2

Welcome Ticket

Use the Welcome Ticket worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 2.

Students: Circle two welcoming choices. Draw or tell how you can welcome someone.

Estudiantes: Encierra dos opciones que dan la bienvenida. Dibuja o di cómo puedes dar la bienvenida.

Take home: My Welcome Ticket
Family chat: What could we say to help someone feel welcome?
Charla en familia: ¿Qué podemos decir para que alguien se sienta bienvenido?

Commitment frame: I can welcome someone by…
Puedo dar la bienvenida a alguien…

Answer notes: Wave hello and offer a place to join are welcoming choices. “Go away” is not.

Tuesday October 27 · Grades K–2

Communication Detective

Use the Communication Detective worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 3.

Students: Circle the ways people communicate. Choose a patient response.

Estudiantes: Encierra las formas en que las personas se comunican. Elige una respuesta paciente.

Take home: Ask and Wait Card
Family chat: People can share ideas in many ways.
Charla en familia: Las personas pueden compartir ideas de muchas formas.

Commitment frame: I can ask, “Would you like help?” Then I wait and listen.
Puedo preguntar: “¿Quieres ayuda?” Luego espero y escucho.

Answer notes: All four are ways to communicate: speech, signs, pictures, and a device. The patient choice is WAIT AND LISTEN.

Wednesday October 28 · Grades K–2

Helping Words

Use the Helping Words worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 4.

Students: Choose safe helping actions. Draw or name a trusted adult.

Estudiantes: Elige acciones seguras para ayudar. Dibuja o nombra a un adulto de confianza.

Take home: My Trusted Adult Card
Family chat: Who can help when someone uses hurtful words?
Charla en familia: ¿Quién puede ayudar cuando alguien usa palabras hirientes?

Commitment frame: “Please help. Someone is being unkind.”
“Por favor, ayúdame. Alguien está siendo poco amable.”

Answer notes: Safe choices: tell a trusted adult, and say “Please stop” if it is safe. Laughing along is not a safe choice.

Thursday October 29 · Grades K–2

Match the Support

Use the Match the Support worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 5.

Students: Match each situation to a helpful support. Tell how one support helps.

Estudiantes: Une cada situación con un apoyo útil. Di cómo ayuda un apoyo.

Take home: My Support Finder
Family chat: A disability is not always something we can see.
Charla en familia: Una discapacidad no siempre es algo que podemos ver.

Commitment frame: A support that helps someone is…
Un apoyo que ayuda a alguien es…

Answer notes: 1-C: a room feels too loud, a quieter space. 2-A: someone uses pictures to talk, a communication board. 3-B: a reader needs words spoken, an audiobook.
