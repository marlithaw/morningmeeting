Disability Awareness Week

October 26–30 is Disability Awareness Week at Matchbook. Each day pairs a short video and key message with a discussion, a grade-band worksheet, and a take-home card.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Friday, October 30: Kindness into Action runs as Friday’s Morning Spark, followed by Friday Cypher.
