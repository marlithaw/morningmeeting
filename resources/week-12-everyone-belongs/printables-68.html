Monday October 26 · Grades 6–8

Belonging in Real Life

Use the Belonging in Real Life worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 12.

Students: Choose two useful changes. Write an invitation that respects the peer’s choice.

Estudiantes: Elige dos cambios útiles. Escribe una invitación que respete la decisión del compañero.

Take home: Belonging Beyond School
Family chat: Why can’t appearance tell us all of someone’s needs?
Charla en familia: ¿Por qué la apariencia no nos dice todas las necesidades de alguien?

Commitment frame: Our group could change… My invitation: …
Nuestro grupo podría cambiar… Mi invitación: …

Answer notes: Useful changes: allow more response time and offer different ways to contribute. “Assign no meaningful role” is not useful. A respectful invitation offers, then accepts the answer.

Tuesday October 27 · Grades 6–8

Myth Busters

Use the Myth Busters worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 13.

Students: Choose fact or myth. Explain one answer using what you learned.

Estudiantes: Elige hecho o mito. Explica una respuesta con lo que aprendiste.

Take home: Explain It Simply
Family chat: Why might two learners use different tools?
Charla en familia: ¿Por qué dos estudiantes podrían usar herramientas diferentes?

Commitment frame: One accurate idea I can share is…
Una idea correcta que puedo compartir es…

Answer notes: On the worksheet: reading difficulty tells us intelligence: MYTH. Audio and other tools can support access: FACT. Everyone with the same disability needs identical help: MYTH.

Wednesday October 28 · Grades 6–8

Speak Up Plan

Use the Speak Up Plan worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 14.

Students: Write a safe thing you could say. Write a way to support the peer. Name a trusted adult you would tell.

Estudiantes: Escribe algo seguro que podrías decir. Escribe una forma de apoyar al compañero. Nombra a un adulto de confianza a quien le dirías.

Take home: My Support Plan
Family chat: What can you do when a peer is mocked online or in person?
Charla en familia: ¿Qué puedes hacer cuando se burlan de un compañero en línea o en persona?

Commitment frame: If I see this, I will… then I will tell…
Si veo esto, voy a… y luego le diré a…

Answer notes: A complete plan has three parts: a safe interruption (“Not cool. Let’s go.”), a supportive check-in with the peer, and a report to a named adult. Online: do not forward or join in.

Thursday October 29 · Grades 6–8

Remove a Barrier

Use the Remove a Barrier worksheet: Disability Awareness packet, page 15.

Students: Identify the barrier and a possible change. Explain who could benefit.

Estudiantes: Identifica la barrera y un posible cambio. Explica quién podría beneficiarse.

Take home: Access at Home
Family chat: How can one small change make an activity more accessible?
Charla en familia: ¿Cómo puede un pequeño cambio hacer una actividad más accesible?

Commitment frame: Barrier: … Change: … Benefits: …
Barrera: … Cambio: … Beneficia a: …

Answer notes: Missing captions are the barrier. Captions or a text transcript are possible changes. They help people who are deaf or hard of hearing, and also anyone in a noisy room, learning English, or who wants to reread the steps.
