Matchbook Morning Meeting · LiveSchoolGrades K–2 · 10

Slide 1 · Launch · 0:30

Today we will explore LiveSchool together. We will find point information, talk about green and red points, and connect our hard work and growth to our school store.

Teacher move

Before projecting, open a school-approved demonstration account or a de-identified example. Have the student view ready in a separate tab. Preview which points and labels your school displays.

Look for

Students know this is a guided practice with feedback, not a comparison of classmates.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 2 · Regulate · 0:30

Let us settle before we look at feedback. Take two comfortable breaths with me. You may keep breathing normally and simply feel your feet on the floor.

Teacher move

Model two gentle breaths. Offer a quiet gaze or feet-on-floor option.

Look for

Bodies settle without requiring everyone to use the same posture.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 3 · Orient · 1:00

A point records information about a choice. We use that information to notice what is working and what needs practice. Your worth and your place in our community do not depend on a number.

Teacher move

Introduce the route. Explain that teacher and student screens can look different. Everyone can follow by watching.

Look for

Students can name what they will learn to find.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 4 · Connect · 1:00

Think of a time you kept working, asked for help, or tried again. What changed because you practiced? We want to notice that kind of growth at school.

Teacher move

Give 15 seconds to think, then a brief partner turn. Accept an example outside school. Invite one response about effort.

Look for

Students name a specific effort or improvement rather than a point total.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 5 · Practice · Points · 2:00

Green points record positive behaviors adults recognize. Red points record behavior concerns that need attention or practice. We look at the reason for an entry. A red entry is a moment to understand and respond to. You can still make a helpful next choice.

Teacher move

In the teacher Points screen, show only a demonstration record. Identify the green and red values and the selected timeframe. Read an actual school behavior label for each color. If using student view only, locate a positive and negative Timeline entry. Do not promise that every student screen shows teacher-style point bubbles.

Look for

Students connect each color to recorded behavior and can describe a constructive response.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 6 · Practice · Timeline · 2:00

Now we will look at the information behind a point. Read the behavior, the date, and any comment that appears. A comment can help explain what the adult noticed. If you are confused, ask the teacher privately and respectfully.

Teacher move

Switch to the prepared student view. Open Timeline. Locate one example and read the visible date, behavior, point value, and comment if present. Return to the main menu or dashboard. If no comment appears, explain that comments are optional. Ask a student to describe the route back to that entry.

Look for

Students can find the entry and use its details before making an assumption.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 7 · Practice · Balance · 3:00

Our point history shows recorded choices. Our spending balance tells us what is available to use. These numbers may differ. We read the balance on the screen rather than guessing from the green number.

Teacher move

Point to the balance in student view. Explain the school’s verified rule for how red points affect spending. LiveSchool lets schools choose whether negative points reduce the balance, so confirm this before teaching. In the teacher view, the green/red timeframe can change while the saved balance remains separate. Model returning to the dashboard, finding the balance, and reopening Timeline. Have students direct your clicks or practice on their own approved account. Do not open the store catalog or make a purchase.

Look for

Students identify the available balance and can distinguish it from green/red history.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 8 · Practice · School store · 2:00

The school store is one way our school acknowledges the effort you put into learning, practicing our expectations, and growing. Our school counselors run it during lunch periods. We want you to understand the work behind the points and feel proud of your progress.

Teacher move

Explain only the confirmed arrangement: schoolwide store, lunch periods, school counselors. Tell students to follow the announced visit directions and ask a counselor for help with the store process. Do not invent open days, thresholds, deduction rules, or eligibility conditions. Ask students to connect a real action to growth.

Look for

Students explain that the store acknowledges effort and growth and identify counselors as the adults who run it.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 9 · Commit · 2:00

In this example, what needed to change? What showed growth afterward? Choose one action you can practice today. Make it something you can actually do, even when the moment is hard.

Teacher move

Allow a short partner response to the scenario. Give students time to say, write, or point to a commitment. If they say “get more points,” ask what action they will practice. Avoid promising a point for every action.

Look for

Each commitment names an observable behavior rather than a score target.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.

Slide 10 · Transition · 1:00

Show me or tell me how you would find your feedback. Tell me why we have the school store. Carry your commitment into our next lesson. Your next choice is another chance to practice.

Teacher move

Take two brief responses. Clarify: Timeline helps explain points, balance shows available spending points, and counselors run the store during lunch to acknowledge hard work and growth. Have students sign out or close their own account as appropriate and transition to the next task.

Look for

Students can locate feedback, explain the store’s purpose, and begin the next routine.

Access and support

Read each label aloud. Let children point, gesture, or echo a phrase. Keep the walkthrough teacher-led. Students do not need their own devices.
