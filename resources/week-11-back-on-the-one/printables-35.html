Monday October 19 · Grades 3–5

Our Place. Our People.

The moment

Nia returns quietly. Jay asks, “Tell us everything!”

A different move

Jay tries: “Good morning, Nia. Your place is here.”

What changes

Nia chooses a wave. Jay accepts her choice.

See it / Name it

Which welcome kept Nia in control of her own story?

Worked response: Partners use names, offer a choice, and accept a quiet response.

Do it

Welcome Remix: pairs try three welcome options: a spoken greeting, a gesture, and an offer of space. Listener chooses one. Switch. Add a quiet returner: welcome them without asking what happened over break.

My commitment

At ____, I will welcome someone by ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Tuesday October 20 · Grades 3–5

Listen for the Whole Beat

The moment

Ari gives two steps. Bo starts answering before Ari finishes.

A different move

Bo pauses: “Let me check what I heard.”

What changes

Ari confirms. Bo responds to the whole message.

See it / Name it

What changed when Bo checked instead of guessing?

Worked response: The listener restates key details and clarifies before responding.

Do it

Message Builder: A describes a red circle with a dot inside and a blue square below it. B listens fully, restates the steps, asks one clarifying question, then draws. Switch with a triangle above a green line. Compare the message with the product.

My commitment

Before I respond during ____, I will check ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Wednesday October 21 · Grades 3–5

A Reset Is a Way Back In

The moment

Sam says, “I missed the first step.”

A different move

An adult offers: “Let me show you one part.”

What changes

Sam tries that part with support.

See it / Name it

Where did support make rejoining possible?

Worked response: The next step is specific, supported when needed, and actually attempted.

Do it

Reset-to-Rejoin Lab: partners use a fictional hard task. Rehearse notice → settle → signal → one part. Partner acting as the adult offers a read-aloud or a first-step model. Switch. Explain how the support helps access the task.

My commitment

If ____ happens in ____, I will signal for ____ and begin ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Thursday October 22 · Grades 3–5

Make Room in the Cypher

The moment

Lee notices two voices filling the group. Ren has no turn.

A different move

Lee asks Ren, “Point, tell us, or pass?”

What changes

The group waits and uses the idea Ren offers.

See it / Name it

How did the group use Ren’s contribution?

Worked response: Every student receives an accessible invitation; the product uses all three clues.

Do it

Three-Clue Build: groups of three get clues: repeat circle-triangle; use six shapes; start with a circle. Each member may read, point, dictate, or pass while an adult reads. Invite each contribution, wait, then draw the shared six-shape pattern.

My commitment

During ____, I will invite an idea by saying ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.
