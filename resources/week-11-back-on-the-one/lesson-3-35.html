Screen 1 · Welcome · 0:00

Target: Write a goal for after break and the first step toward it.

Script: "Monday we looked back. Yesterday we made a plan for the break. Today we get ready for the day we come back. The first morning after a break sets the tone for the whole next stretch of the year. We are going to decide right now what that morning looks like." Look for: Students connect the three days. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 2 · Regulate · 2:00

Prepare: One Comeback Card per student and the Comeback Folder label.

Script: "Feet flat on the floor. Feel the chair holding you. Breathe in for four. Out for six. The long breath out is the one that calms you. Three rounds. This is your reset. The first morning back after break, your body may feel tired or jittery. This is what you will do." Look for: Bodies still by the third round. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 3 · Orient · 2:00

Model "Ready body: in bed by 9 the night before. Ready routine: First Five, no reminders. Ready goal: I want to get better at multiplication facts. First step: practice 10 minutes a day in IXL the first week back."

Script: "Coming back ready has three parts. Ready Body: you slept, and you ate. Breakfast is at school every morning if you need it. Ready Routine: you remember the First Five without a reminder. Ready Goal: you know one thing you are working on."

Check: "If you forget the First Five after break, what do you do?" Expected: Look at the board and start. Do my best and keep going. Look for: Students name all three parts. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 4 · Connect · Look · 2:00

Worked response "Omar remembered the First Five. Eli was still on break in his head. Eli can save the break stories for Morning Meeting or lunch, look at the board, and start."

If a student is unsure: "Omar _. Eli can _."

Script: "Look at the first morning back. Don't decide who is a good student. Look at what each person does." Give twenty seconds of silent looking, then take two or three answers. Facilitation: Students will want to share break stories when they return. Name where those belong: Morning Meeting and lunch. Look for: Students use the First Five steps as evidence. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 5 · Connect · Share · 1:00

Response options Academic or Morning Meeting goals both count.

Script: "Think about your Next from Monday's Look Back Card. That might be your goal. Partner A, then Partner B. A pass is okay." Look for: Goals connect to Monday's Next. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 6 · Practice · 3:00

Three-minute facilitation 0:00 to 0:30: point to the goal and first step. 0:30 to 2:30: students complete the card. 2:30 to 3:00: partners read their goal and first step.

Look for: Goals are specific. First steps happen in the first week back. If the response misses the skill: If the goal is "do better," ask, "Better at what? What is your first step?"

Script: "This is your Comeback Card. It stays here at school in our Comeback Folder. On our first morning back, you get it back so you walk in remembering your goal." Facilitation: Collect every card into the Comeback Folder. Store Monday's Look Back Cards in the same folder. Look for: Every card has a goal and a reset. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 7 · Commit · 3:00

If support is needed: See the response support on the Practice screen. Commitment support: Point to the student's first step on their card. Ask, "Could you do a small part of that today?" Extend when ready: "What could get in the way of your goal? What will you do then?"

Script: "Goals do not start after break. They start today. Name one small thing you can do today that moves you toward your goal. Whisper it to your partner." Look for: Commitments are small and doable today. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 8 · Transition · 2:00

Expected exit response "I come back with a ready body, a ready routine, and a goal with a first step."

Mastery evidence: Students can describe their first morning back and name their goal.

Script: "Tomorrow we celebrate. Friday Cypher is on Thursday this week because there is no school Friday. Think about who you saw this week being Safe, Respectful, and Responsible. Nominations close at the end of today. Stand on the one." Look for: A calm transition into the first task. Access: Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.
