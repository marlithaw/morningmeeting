Matchbook Morning Meeting · Back on the OneGrades K–2 · 08

1. Welcome · ready screen

Script: Welcome back. Some of us are ready to talk, and some need a quiet start. Neither changes whether we belong. Our job is to make returning easier: greet, offer space, and respect the response. Watch for the difference between inviting a person in and asking them to perform a happy story.

Look for

Each partner offers a greeting and accepts the other person’s choice.

Access

Offer pointing, drawing, dictation, quiet thought, or a pass. Eyes may stay open; no forced eye contact. Keep break stories optional.

Script: Before play: What did Jay do to help Nia join? Play no more than 60 seconds of the selected clip, then use the fictional three-panel model. Ask students to name an observable action. A correct response names the invitation, verification, support, or participation choice. If video is blocked, the complete pictured model works without it.

5. Connect · Share · 1:00

Script: Give ten seconds to think. Partner A shares for twenty seconds; partner B for twenty seconds. Close by naming one concrete action. Do not ask students to recount private experiences.

6. Practice · Do It · 3:00

Script: Prepare: blank paper and pencil. Welcome Relay: stay in your own space. Partner A says “Good morning, ____. Your place is here.” Partner B chooses a wave, nod, or hello. Switch. Try again with one person choosing quiet. Run 30 seconds of adult modeling, 60 seconds of first rehearsal, 20 seconds of specific feedback, 60 seconds to switch or retry, and ten seconds to name the move. Correct gently: Offer a greeting; let the other person choose the response.

Look for

Each partner offers a greeting and accepts the other person’s choice.

Access

Offer pointing, drawing, dictation, quiet thought, or a pass. Eyes may stay open; no forced eye contact. Keep break stories optional.

7. Commit · 3:00

Script: Students complete the dated toolkit card. Give 90 seconds to choose a setting and action, 45 seconds to draw or write, and 45 seconds for an optional partner check. On Monday, privately reconnect to the pre-break Comeback Card if it is available. New or absent students choose today’s commitment. No student needs a prior card to participate.
