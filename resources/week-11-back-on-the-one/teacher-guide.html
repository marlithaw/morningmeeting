Monday October 19

Our Place. Our People.

Grades K–2

Present Print cards

1. Welcome · ready screen

Script: Welcome back. Some of us are ready to talk, and some need a quiet start. Neither changes whether we belong. Our job is to make returning easier: greet, offer space, and respect the response. Watch for the difference between inviting a person in and asking them to perform a happy story.

Look for

Each partner offers a greeting and accepts the other person’s choice.

Access

Offer pointing, drawing, dictation, quiet thought, or a pass. Eyes may stay open; no forced eye contact. Keep break stories optional.

6. Practice · Do It · 3:00

Script: Prepare: blank paper and pencil. Welcome Relay: stay in your own space. Partner A says “Good morning, ____. Your place is here.” Partner B chooses a wave, nod, or hello. Switch. Try again with one person choosing quiet. Run 30 seconds of adult modeling, 60 seconds of first rehearsal, 20 seconds of specific feedback, 60 seconds to switch or retry, and ten seconds to name the move. Correct gently: Offer a greeting; let the other person choose the response.
