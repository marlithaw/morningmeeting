Monday October 19 · Grades K–2

Our Place. Our People.

The moment

Nia returns quietly. Jay asks, “Tell us everything!”

A different move

Jay tries: “Good morning, Nia. Your place is here.”

What changes

Nia chooses a wave. Jay accepts her choice.

See it / Name it

What did Jay do to help Nia join?

Worked response: Each partner offers a greeting and accepts the other person’s choice.

Do it

Welcome Relay: stay in your own space. Partner A says “Good morning, ____. Your place is here.” Partner B chooses a wave, nod, or hello. Switch. Try again with one person choosing quiet.

My commitment

At our next group time, I will make room by ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Tuesday October 20 · Grades K–2

Listen for the Whole Beat

The moment

Ari gives two steps. Bo starts answering before Ari finishes.

A different move

Bo pauses: “Let me check what I heard.”

What changes

Ari confirms. Bo responds to the whole message.

See it / Name it

How did Bo check the message?

Worked response: Both students wait, check the message, and complete two steps.

Do it

Draw-and-Check: A says “Draw a circle. Put a dot inside.” B waits, repeats or points to the two steps, then draws. Switch: draw a square, then a line underneath. Compare kindly.

My commitment

When someone gives directions today, I will ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Wednesday October 21 · Grades K–2

A Reset Is a Way Back In

The moment

Sam says, “I missed the first step.”

A different move

An adult offers: “Let me show you one part.”

What changes

Sam tries that part with support.

See it / Name it

What helped Sam begin again?

Worked response: A student uses a reset or help signal and completes one supported action.

Do it

Back-in Move: teacher models a stuck moment. Students keep hands resting, settle comfortably, and use the class help signal. Teacher offers “circle the first word or point to the first picture.” Students do one part. Repeat with pointing instead of words.

My commitment

If I get stuck in ____, I can ask for help and ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.

Thursday October 22 · Grades K–2

Make Room in the Cypher

The moment

Lee notices two voices filling the group. Ren has no turn.

A different move

Lee asks Ren, “Point, tell us, or pass?”

What changes

The group waits and uses the idea Ren offers.

See it / Name it

How did Lee make room for Ren?

Worked response: Partners offer a turn, wait, and accept a response or pass.

Do it

Clue Circle: teacher shows circle, triangle, circle, triangle, blank. Each partner gets a choice to point, talk, or pass. Wait three quiet seconds before a response. Build the next two shapes together.

My commitment

During ____, I will give someone space to ____.

Say, point, draw, dictate, or write. Private stories stay private.
