Monday October 5 · Grades K–2 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

The Year So Far picture strip (toolkit) and one Look Back Card per student. Crayons.

Say and model

Teacher points to each picture: "I am proud our class can do the flower breath. We learned how to say 'Can I?' before we touch. Next, we are still growing at starting our Do Now fast."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 1:00: hold up the card and point to the star, light bulb, and seedling. 1:00 to 2:30: students draw in the star box and circle one picture in the light bulb box. 2:30 to 3:00: partners show their star drawing and say "I am proud I can ____."

Worked answer

"I learned to breathe like a flower when I am mad." "I learned to help a friend." "I learned to ask before I hug."

Response scaffold

Point to a picture. An adult names it: "That is the helping hand. Do you remember that one?"

Look for

Every box has something. Proud lines are specific. Next lines are honest.

If the response misses the skill

If a child draws something unrelated, ask, "What can you do now in that picture?" Accept the answer they give.

Extend when ready

"What is one tool from this year you could teach to someone at home?"

Expected exit response

"I learned a lot, and I am proud I can ____."

Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Tuesday October 6 · Grades K–2 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

One My Break Plan card per student (picture choices) and one Calm Tools to Take Home card per student. Crayons.

Say and model

Teacher points to each picture: "Rest: I will go to bed when it gets dark. Safe: I will stay where my grown-up can find me. Respectful: I will share with my family. Responsible: I will read a book."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 0:45: hold up the card and point to each box. 0:45 to 2:30: students circle one picture in each box. 2:30 to 3:00: partners show one circled picture and say "I will ____."

Worked answer

"Maya rests. Maya reads. Maya helps." Point to each picture and match it to Rest, Responsible, Respectful.

Response scaffold

Point to a picture. "What is Maya doing?" Then point to the matching plan word.

Look for

Actions are concrete. 6–8 lines have a time or place.

If the response misses the skill

If a child circles everything in a box, say, "Pick the one you will do first." Accept it.

Extend when ready

"Which part of your plan will be hardest to keep? What will help?"

Expected exit response

"My plan has rest, safe, respectful, and responsible."

Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Wednesday October 7 · Grades K–2 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

One Comeback Card per student, the First Five picture strip from Week 9 (already posted), and the Comeback Folder label (toolkit).

Say and model

Teacher points: "Ready body: I go to bed early the night before. Ready routine: come in, sit down, get my things, start. Ready goal: I want to get better at reading words."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 0:45: point to each box and model. 0:45 to 2:30: students draw their goal and circle their reset. 2:30 to 3:00: students point to the First Five pictures in order with a partner.

Worked answer

"Eli can look at the pictures. Eli can watch a friend. Eli can come in and sit down."

Response scaffold

Point to the First Five pictures. "What does Omar do first?"

Look for

Goals are specific. First steps happen in the first week back.

If the response misses the skill

If a child draws something unrelated, ask, "What do you want to get better at?" Write their words under the drawing.

Extend when ready

"What could get in the way of your goal? What will you do then?"

Expected exit response

"When I come back, I come in, sit down, get my things, and start. I want to get better at ____."

Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.
