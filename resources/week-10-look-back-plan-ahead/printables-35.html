Monday October 5 · Grades 3–5 · Student tool

Look Back Card

Name: __________________________

PROUD

I am proud that I ____.

LEARNED

A tool I use is ____. I used it when ____.

NEXT

I am still working on ____.

Today I will use ____.

Point, say, draw, or tell an adult.

Monday October 5 · Grades 3–5 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

The Year So Far timeline card (toolkit) and one Look Back Card per student.

Say and model

"Proud: our class starts Morning Meeting faster than in August. Learned: we use Ask → Wait → Respect. Next: we are still working on keeping going during the Do Now without reminders."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 0:30: point to the three boxes. 0:30 to 2:30: students complete the card. 2:30 to 3:00: partners read their Proud line to each other.

Worked answer

"I used five-finger breathing at home when my brother made me mad." "I used Ask → Wait → Respect at recess before I joined a game." "I used the First Five in science yesterday."

Response scaffold

"One tool I have used is _. I used it when _."

Look for

Every box has something. Proud lines are specific. Next lines are honest.

If the response misses the skill

If a student writes "I learned a lot," ask, "Name one thing. When did you use it?"

Extend when ready

"What is one tool from this year you could teach to someone at home?"

Expected exit response

"I can name a tool I use and something I am still working on."

Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Tuesday October 6 · Grades 3–5 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

One My Break Plan card and one Calm Tools to Take Home card per student.

Say and model

"Rest: sleep by 9:30. Safe: use five-finger breathing when I get upset. Respectful: help cook dinner without being asked. Responsible: read 15 minutes before bed."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 0:30: point to the "someone could see" line. 0:30 to 2:30: students write one action in each box. 2:30 to 3:00: partners check one box for each other: "Could someone see that?"

Worked answer

"Lin's plan will happen because each part is something you can see and has a time. Omar's plan has no action. Lin's plan also counts watching her cousin. Helping your family is Responsible."

Response scaffold

"Lin's plan has ____. Omar's plan does not."

Look for

Actions are concrete. 6–8 lines have a time or place.

If the response misses the skill

If a box says "be good," ask, "What would being good look like? What would someone see you doing?"

Extend when ready

"Which part of your plan will be hardest to keep? What will help?"

Expected exit response

"A plan names an action someone could see. A wish does not."

Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Wednesday October 7 · Grades 3–5 · Teacher companion

Model · Prompt · Check

Have ready

One Comeback Card per student and the Comeback Folder label.

Say and model

"Ready body: in bed by 9 the night before. Ready routine: First Five, no reminders. Ready goal: I want to get better at multiplication facts. First step: practice 10 minutes a day in IXL the first week back."

Three-minute practice

0:00 to 0:30: point to the goal and first step. 0:30 to 2:30: students complete the card. 2:30 to 3:00: partners read their goal and first step.

Worked answer

"Omar remembered the First Five. Eli was still on break in his head. Eli can save the break stories for Morning Meeting or lunch, look at the board, and start."

Response scaffold

"Omar _. Eli can _."

Look for

Goals are specific. First steps happen in the first week back.

If the response misses the skill

If the goal is "do better," ask, "Better at what? What is your first step?"

Extend when ready

"What could get in the way of your goal? What will you do then?"

Expected exit response

"I come back with a ready body, a ready routine, and a goal with a first step."

Offer spoken and written frames. Give quiet thinking time. Students may pass. Ask for a specific detail.

Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.
