Plan & prepare

Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 = 15 minutes. The title is a ready screen. N opens notes on the projected screen; use this guide on a separate device when possible. Thursday is the Friday Cypher Celebration this week. No school Friday.

  1. Print before Monday: Look Back Cards. Before Tuesday: My Break Plan cards and Calm Tools to Take Home cards. Before Wednesday: Comeback Cards and the Comeback Folder label.
  2. Look Back Cards and Comeback Cards stay at school in the Comeback Folder. My Break Plan and Calm Tools cards go home Tuesday.
  3. Breaks look different for every family. Some families travel, some work, some stay home. Do not ask where students are going or what they are getting. Every plan counts.
  4. Reflection is about growth, not grades. Accept academic, social, and Morning Meeting answers equally.
  5. "Still working on it" is a strong answer. Treat Next and growth-edge answers as goals, not confessions.
  6. Students may keep their cards private. Sharing is a choice.
  7. Breakfast is served at school every morning. Name it when teaching Ready Body so no student hears that breakfast depends on home.
  8. Nominations for Thursday's Cypher close at the end of the day Wednesday.

Look How Far We've Come

Grades K–2

Student slidesDaily toolkit

Students will: Name one thing they learned and one thing they are proud they can do.

Have ready: The Year So Far picture strip (toolkit) and one Look Back Card per student. Crayons.

Successful response: The student points to or names one picture from the year and finishes "I am proud I can ____."

Screen 1 · Welcome · 0:00

On screen: LOOK HOW FAR WE'VE COME · We learned so much!

Target: Name one thing they learned and one thing they are proud they can do.

Script: "Good morning. In a few days, we pause for fall break. Before we pause, we are going to do something that strong teams and strong learners do. We look back. We have been in Morning Meeting together since August, and we have learned a lot. This week is three mornings: look back, make a plan, and get ready to come back strong." Look for: Students understand this week is about their own growth, not a test. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 2 · Regulate · 2:00

On screen: SMELL THE FLOWER. BLOW THE CANDLE. · 1. Smell the flower. 2. Blow the candle. 3. Three times. · We learned this in August.

Prepare: The Year So Far picture strip (toolkit) and one Look Back Card per student. Crayons.

Script: "Let's use a tool we already know. Smell the flower. Blow out the candle. Slow. Again. One more time. Look at that. You already knew how to do that. That is learning."

Facilitation: Select a brief Relax excerpt if the room needs more settling. Do not stretch Regulate past two minutes. Look for: Students begin the tool with little or no prompting. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 3 · Orient · 2:00

On screen: PROUD → LEARNED → NEXT · Proud: I can do it! (star) · Learned: Now I know it. (light bulb) · Next: I am still growing. (seedling)

Model Teacher points to each picture: "I am proud our class can do the flower breath. We learned how to say 'Can I?' before we touch. Next, we are still growing at starting our Do Now fast."

Script: "Looking back has three parts. Proud means something you can do now. Learned means something new you know. Next means something you are still growing at. Still growing is good. It means you are not done yet."

Check: "Is 'I am still working on it' a bad answer?" Expected: No. It is the Next. Look for: Students can name the three parts. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 4 · Connect · Look · 2:00

On screen: OUR YEAR SO FAR · five pictures in a path: helping hand · calm body · our stories · Ask → Wait → Respect · First Five · Question: Point to one thing we learned.

Worked response "I learned to breathe like a flower when I am mad." "I learned to help a friend." "I learned to ask before I hug."

If a student is unsure: Point to a picture. An adult names it: "That is the helping hand. Do you remember that one?"

Script: "This is our year so far. Every one of these is something this class learned together. Take twenty seconds to look. Do not talk yet. Just look." Pause twenty seconds. Then read the band question and take two or three answers. Facilitation: Give 20 to 30 seconds of silent looking before taking answers. Name the unit each answer connects to. Look for: Students connect a unit to a real moment, not only a name. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 5 · Connect · Share · 1:00

On screen: I am proud I can ____.

Response options Point to a picture on the strip and say "I am proud I can ____." A partner or adult can say it with the student.

Script: "Think first. It can be from Morning Meeting or from any class: reading, math, science, anything. Partner A goes first, then Partner B. A pass is okay." Look for: Specific answers ("I can read chapter books now") over general ones ("I got better"). Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 6 · Practice · 3:00

On screen: MY LOOK BACK CARD · PROUD (star): Draw something you can do now. · LEARNED (light bulb): Circle a picture. · NEXT (seedling): I am still growing at ____.

Three-minute facilitation 0:00 to 1:00: hold up the card and point to the star, light bulb, and seedling. 1:00 to 2:30: students draw in the star box and circle one picture in the light bulb box. 2:30 to 3:00: partners show their star drawing and say "I am proud I can ____."

Look for: Every box has something. Proud lines are specific. Next lines are honest. If the response misses the skill: If a child draws something unrelated, ask, "What can you do now in that picture?" Accept the answer they give.

Script: "Now you will make your own Look Back Card. Proud, Learned, Next. Short is fine. Specific is the goal." Facilitation: Collect cards at the end. They come back on the first day after break. Look for: Students work without needing the directions repeated. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 7 · Commit · 3:00

On screen: ONE ACTION TODAY · Today I will use ____. (pictures: flower breath · Ask → Wait → Respect · helping hand · First Five) · Say it, draw it, or keep it private.

If support is needed: See the response support on the Practice screen. Commitment support: Offer one tool from the year; ask the student to say, draw, dictate, or write when they will use it. Do not require sharing. Extend when ready: "What is one tool from this year you could teach to someone at home?"

Script: "You named what you learned. Now use it today. Choose something specific enough that someone could see it. 'Be good' is a wish. 'Use five-finger breathing before the math quiz' is an action." Look for: Commitments name a tool and a moment. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 8 · Transition · 2:00

My Break Plan

Grades K–2

Student slidesDaily toolkit

Students will: Choose one picture for each part of their break plan.

Have ready: One My Break Plan card per student (picture choices) and one Calm Tools to Take Home card per student. Crayons.

Successful response: The student circles one picture in each box and can say one of their choices aloud.

Screen 1 · Welcome · 0:00

On screen: MY BREAK PLAN · A break is a rest. A plan helps.

Target: Choose one picture for each part of their break plan.

Script: "Yesterday we looked back at how far we have come. Today we look ahead. Fall break is almost here. A break is a pause, not a stop. The people who come back strongest are the ones who had a plan for the pause." Look for: Students connect today to yesterday. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 2 · Regulate · 2:00

On screen: BODY WEATHER · Sunny? Cloudy? Stormy? · Check your body. Take one flower breath.

Prepare: One My Break Plan card per student (picture choices) and one Calm Tools to Take Home card per student. Crayons.

Script: "Let's check our body weather like we learned. Is your body sunny, cloudy, or stormy? Put your hand on the picture in your head. Now one flower breath. Smell. Blow. That is a tool you can use at home, too."

Facilitation: These are the Morning Circle check-in tools from the first week of September. Name them by name. Look for: Students settle and notice their body without being asked to report out. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.

Screen 3 · Orient · 2:00

On screen: MY BREAK PLAN · REST (bed) · SAFE (house with a heart) · RESPECTFUL (two friends) · RESPONSIBLE (book)

Model Teacher points to each picture: "Rest: I will go to bed when it gets dark. Safe: I will stay where my grown-up can find me. Respectful: I will share with my family. Responsible: I will read a book."

Script: "A break plan has four parts. Rest means your body gets sleep and quiet time. Safe means you stay where your grown-up wants you and use your calm tools. Respectful means you are kind to your family and friends. Responsible means you keep learning and you help."

Check: "Is 'Be good over break' a plan?" Expected: No. It is a wish. A plan names an action. Look for: Students can name the four parts. Access: Read aloud. Accept pointing, gesture, drawing, dictation, a partner response, quiet thinking, or a pass.

Practice boundary: Breaks look different for every family. Plans and reflections stay private unless the student chooses to share.
