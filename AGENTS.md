# Morning Meeting unit requirements

Every weekly unit must retain visible Weekly Videos (Relax and Excite) and Do Now – First 5 (Relax and Excite) sections, alongside the grade-band decks and toolkit cards. Use the established links from existing unit rows. Include both columns in every daily row of the Instructional Units menu and a visible section on the unit hub. Topic-specific clips supplement these recurring resources; they never replace them. Preserve the six-phase meeting structure and grade-band toolkit cards. Verify these sections before publishing a unit or a redesign.

## Morning Spark and Ember weekly libraries

Number Spark and Ember consecutively within their own weekly library, separately from Morning Meeting instructional-unit numbers. Use `resources/spark-ember/week-N-topic-slug/` for paired packages, with the week number, focus title, and actual school dates visible on the hub. October 5–8, 2026 is Spark/Ember Week 2. Continue with Week 3 for the next supplied school week; preserve earlier folders and sections.

Add each dated week as a full section above earlier weeks in both root hub tabs. Preserve the main heading, shared timeline, teacher guidance, and all archived weekly entries. A package shortcut alone is not a weekly library entry.

Each weekly section repeats the established focus header, four grade-band cards (K–1, 2–3, 4–5, 6–8), presentation/download and printable links, dated teacher guide, and week-at-a-glance table. Morning Spark follows Monday Check-In, Tuesday Picture of the Day, Wednesday Zoom In, Thursday Would You Rather, and Friday Wonder only when Friday is a school day. Ember lists each day's actual band-specific video link and reflection with a direct board link. Preserve the seated dismissal, regulation fallback, release rail, no-lining-up expectation, existing release order, and numbered Chromebook return.

Use the school calendar to omit non-school days. Pin dated Spark decks to the corresponding week from the current IXL source, retain source-backed Math/Reading codes, and flag missing inputs without inventing codes or operational changes. Keep recurring Weekly Videos and Do Now – First 5 links visible. Check every local link and visually inspect the live tabs and projected decks before declaring publication verified.
