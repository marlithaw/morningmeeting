Cool Heads, Kind Words: Strong Feelings, Safe Choices

This week teaches what to do with a strong feeling before it turns into a hurtful word or an unsafe action. Every feeling is allowed, including anger. Students learn to notice the warning signs in their bodies, use a cool-down tool before they act, solve a problem with an I-statement instead of a put-down or a curse word, and make the strong choice when a conflict heats up: walk away, get an adult, and never hype or join a fight. Calm is a skill, and every student can practice it.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Full week: Morning Meeting runs Monday through Thursday, December 7 to December 10.

Friday, December 11: Friday Wonder runs as Friday’s Morning Spark on why staying calm takes real strength, followed by Friday Cypher.
