Back and Ready: Practice the Routines, Then Show What You Know

This is a week of big changes: the first days back from the Thanksgiving break, Checkpoint 2 testing for grades 3–8, and new rooms and new teachers for grades 2–5. Instead of new ideas, this week is about reps. Students rehearse Safe, Respectful, and Responsible in the places where they matter most (arrival, the hallway, the cafeteria, and the classroom), practice a calm, focused testing routine, carry the same expectations into every room, and finish the week with steady effort. Expectations are taught by practicing them, not by posting them.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Full week: Morning Meeting runs Monday through Thursday, November 30 to December 3.

Monday: the first day back after the Thanksgiving break. Grades 2–5 begin in their new rooms and with their new teachers.

Tuesday, December 1: Checkpoint 2 testing begins for grades 3–8. Morning Meeting comes right before testing and prepares students for it. K–2 Acadience testing happens one student at a time during the day.

K–2 this week: each day pairs with a Lee & Tee lesson (Our Morning Meeting, Right-Size Voice, Things Stay Safe, and Asking for a Break). K–2 rooms teach the Lee & Tee lesson; the K–2 deck teaches the same skill if a room prefers it.

Friday, December 4: Friday Wonder runs as Friday’s Morning Spark on effort and finishing strong, followed by Friday Cypher.
