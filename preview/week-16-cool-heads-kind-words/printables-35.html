Circle your warning signs: hot face · tight fists · racing heart · loud voice · tight jaw · other:

Wednesday December 2 · Grades 3–5

Flip It to an I-Statement

Name: _____________________

Students: Flip two you-messages into I-statements. Practice one with a partner in a calm voice.

Estudiantes: Cambia dos mensajes con “tú” a mensajes en primera persona. Practica uno con un compañero con voz tranquila.

“You never let me play!” → I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.
“You’re cheating!” → I feel ___ when ___. I need ___.
I practiced with my partner in a calm voice: ☐

My commitment: An I-statement I can use this week is…
Un mensaje en primera persona que puedo usar esta semana es…

Take home: I-Statements
Family chat: How do people in our family work out a disagreement?
Charla en familia: ¿Cómo resuelven los desacuerdos en nuestra familia?

Point, say, draw, dictate, or write.
