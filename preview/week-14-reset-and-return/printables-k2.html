Monday November 9 · Grades K–2

Help or Stop?

Name: _____________________

Students: Color the pictures that help learning green. Draw one way you wait.

Estudiantes: Colorea de verde los dibujos que ayudan a aprender. Dibuja una forma en que esperas.

Color green what helps learning: Raise my hand · Breathe and wait · Shout out
Draw one way you wait.

My commitment: When I want to shout, I can…
Cuando quiero gritar, puedo…

Take home: Waiting at Home
Family chat: What helps you wait for your turn at home?
Charla en familia: ¿Qué te ayuda a esperar tu turno en casa?

Point, say, draw, dictate, or write.

Wednesday November 11 · Grades K–2

Practice Reflection: Leo

Name: _____________________

Students: Circle a feeling for Leo and who missed learning. Circle Leo’s next move.

Estudiantes: Encierra un sentimiento para Leo y quién no aprendió. Encierra el siguiente paso de Leo.

How does Leo feel? Circle one: Mad · Sad · Worried · Confused · Bored · Tired · Wiggly
Who missed learning? Circle all: Leo · His friends · His class · His teacher
Leo’s next move. Circle one: Eyes on my teacher · Voice off · Pencil working · Hands to myself

My commitment: A next move that helps me learn is…
Un siguiente paso que me ayuda a aprender es…

Take home: My Next Move
Family chat: What is a good next move after a mistake?
Charla en familia: ¿Cuál es un buen siguiente paso después de un error?

Point, say, draw, dictate, or write.
