Reset and Return: How Our Classrooms Respond

This week teaches students the classroom response process every teacher now uses. Every behavior has a root, and in the moment the response is to the impact on learning. Level 1 happens in the classroom: a warning, a seat change, then a written reflection. Level 2 is Tap In support and Tap Out. Level 3 is administrative and goes to the Restorative Room. Safety always comes first. Removal is sometimes the outcome; returning to learning is always the goal.

Six phases: Regulate 2 · Orient 2 · Connect 3 · Practice 3 · Commit 3 · Transition 2 minutes. Welcome is the ready screen.

Full week: Morning Meeting runs Monday through Thursday, November 9–12.

Wednesday: students practice the Reset and Return written reflection for their grade band with a story about a fictional student. The toolkit practice page follows the same five questions as the written reflection, which is available in English and Spanish.

Friday, November 13: Friday Wonder runs as Friday’s Morning Spark on bouncing back, followed by Friday Cypher.
