# Calm Corner song: two lyric options (draft)

Same frame as Gentle Hands: intro call, a hook with echo parts in parentheses (they show in yellow in the captions),
the four calm corner steps, a call-and-response, and the promise as the last line.
Target length: about 1 minute 20 seconds. Key words: big feeling, notice, calm corner, calm tool, come back.

---

## Option A: "Get Calm, Come Back" (bouncy, like Gentle Hands)

Suno style: upbeat kids hip hop, 92 BPM, bouncy drums, handclaps, kids call and response, warm rap vocals, positive classroom energy

[Intro]
Matchbook, are you ready? (Ready!)
Big feelings come, big feelings go,
Let's learn it, let's go!

[Hook]
Calm corner (calm corner!) is a place to rest,
I get calm (I get calm!), then I'm at my best.
Notice, walk, get calm, come back,
Calm corner keeps me right on track!

[Verse 1 - Lee]
My tower fell down and my face got hot,
My hands got tight, my tummy in a knot.
I noticed my feeling, I gave it a name,
Big feelings are okay, nobody's to blame.

[Verse 2 - Tee]
I walk, don't run, to the cozy spot,
One friend at a time, that's the deal we got.
I hug a soft friend, I read a book,
I find my feeling and I take a look.
Smell the flower (sniff!), blow the candle (whoo!),
My body feels calm, now I know what to do.

[Hook]
Calm corner (calm corner!) is a place to rest,
I get calm (I get calm!), then I'm at my best.
Notice, walk, get calm, come back,
Calm corner keeps me right on track!

[Bridge - call and response]
Is the calm corner a time-out? (No!)
Is it a place to play? (No!)
Is it a place to get calm? (Yes!)
Then I come back to stay! (Yes!)

[Outro]
Thumbs up, I'm ready, back to the rug,
Hand on your heart, give yourself a hug.
Say it with me:
I get calm. Then I come back to learn.

## Option B: "My Calm Corner" (soft groove, models calm)

Suno style: gentle kids R&B hip hop, 80 BPM, soft piano, finger snaps, warm lo-fi drums, soothing vocals, calm cozy mood

[Intro]
Matchbook, breathe with me. (Breathe!)
In through your nose,
Out, slow and free.

[Hook]
When it's too big, too loud, too much,
I've got a place with a soft, calm touch.
Calm corner (calm corner!), I go there to rest,
I get calm (I get calm!), I come back, I do my best.

[Verse 1 - Tee]
My work felt hard and I wanted to cry,
My chest got tight and I didn't know why.
I didn't run, I walked nice and slow,
To the cozy corner where the soft lights glow.

[Verse 2 - Lee]
I hugged a soft bear, I opened a book,
I pointed to "worried" and I took a good look.
Smell the flower (mmm), blow the candle (whoo),
Now my body says ready, and I know what to do.

[Hook]
When it's too big, too loud, too much,
I've got a place with a soft, calm touch.
Calm corner (calm corner!), I go there to rest,
I get calm (I get calm!), I come back, I do my best.

[Bridge - echo the steps]
Notice (notice!), walk (walk!),
Get calm (get calm!), come back (come back!).

[Outro]
Hand on your heart, say it with me:
I get calm. Then I come back to learn.
