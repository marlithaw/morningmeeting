# Calm Corner song (draft 2: shared intro and chorus)

The intro and the "I can choose" chorus are the same words as Gentle Hands (see ../SHARED.md).
New for this topic: the hook, the two verses, and the outro promise.

[Intro]
Matchbook, are you ready? (Ready!)
Hands up high, hands down low,
Let's learn it, let's go!

[Hook]
Calm corner (calm corner!) is where I rest,
I get calm (I get calm!), then I'm at my best.
Notice, walk, get calm, come back,
Calm corner keeps me right on track!

[Verse 1]
My tower fell down and my face got hot,
My hands got tight, my tummy in a knot.
I noticed my feeling, I gave it a name,
Big feelings are okay, nobody's to blame.
I walk, don't run, to the cozy spot,
One friend at a time, that's the deal we got.

[Chorus - Calm Choices]
When I feel mad, I can choose! (I can choose!)
Breathe in slow, blow it out, whoo!
Calm corner, break card, use my words,
Ask a grown-up, I get help, that's how it works!

[Verse 2]
I hug a soft friend, I read a book,
I find my feeling and I take a look.
My body feels calm, my breathing is slow,
Back to the rug, now I'm ready to go!

[Chorus - Calm Choices]
When I feel mad, I can choose! (I can choose!)
Breathe in slow, blow it out, whoo!
Calm corner, break card, use my words,
Ask a grown-up, I get help, that's how it works!

[Outro]
Big feelings! (I use the calm corner!)
Hand on your heart, say it with me:
I get calm. Then I come back to learn.
